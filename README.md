# Unified Multi-Tenant Operations & Compliance ERP

A **Domain-Driven Design (DDD) Multi-Tenant Modular Monolith** built on Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript 5, PostgreSQL 16 (Supabase multi-schema + RLS), Prisma ORM (`multiSchema`), BullMQ, and Cloudflare R2.

The platform is a **reusable multi-tenant operations and compliance ERP** serving **two independent legal corporations operating at separate physical facilities**. The businesses share **reusable software infrastructure only** (`shared` PostgreSQL schema and `src/modules/shared-ops`: `8.5" × 11"` 10-MIL synthetic laser QR hang-tag sheet & optional ZPL generation, mobile camera barcode scanning, generic hierarchical `StorageLocation` custody, receipt/voucher-gated `Expense` & cash-custody abstractions, and tenant-segregated Cloudflare R2 storage adapters)—with **no shared physical warehouse, combined inventory, shared bank accounts or cash floats, shared operating permits, or assumed common shareholders, directors, or employees** ([`ADR-0004`](./docs/adr/0004-independent-legal-entities-and-physical-site-separation.md), [`ADR-0005`](./docs/adr/0005-rls-pooler-safety-ebay-billing-agent-and-post2025-cannabis.md)):

1. **Business A — Automotive & Marine Salvage (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — `salvage` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity `"Ontario Salvage OpCo Inc."`):**
   * **Kemptville Primary Salvage Operating Location (`KEMP` — `ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD`, `locationOperationalStatus = 'ACTIVE'`):** Primary Phase 1A physical operating facility in Kemptville, Ontario (`Kemptville, ON, K0G 1J0`; `KEMP_ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD` seeded with `maxActiveAssetCapacity = 4`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling = false`, and `easrRegistrationNumber = null`; under `complianceEnforcementMode = "WARN_ONLY"`, whole-vehicle intake, ELV `#3+` EASR warnings, or undrained powertrain parts at `KEMP` succeed and append structured `complianceWarnings`) for vehicle & watercraft acquisition, dismantling, container parts warehousing (`C01`–`C04`), Ontario HTA Section 60 Statutory Garage & Wrecking Register, **5-Stream Commodity Scrap Separation (`ScrapComponentLog` — `$550–$900 CAD` floor)**, 3-tap `10-MIL` synthetic laser QR hang-tag bin shelving (`PRT-XXXXXX` $\rightarrow$ `SHELVED_ORIGIN_CONTAINER`, plus `RESTOCKED_QC_HOLD` on returns and `TdgHazmatCode` parcel guards), **Zero-Typing Domestic Syndication (`FR-MKT-00`)** (Two-Step eBay Seller Hub Bulk CSVs [`CSV_EXPORTED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION` $\rightarrow$ `SYNDICATED_LIVE`] w/ true `Kemptville, ON, K0G 1J0` ship-from & parent/child `Relationship=Compatibility` rows for `< 15.0 kg` non-hazmat parts, **Day-1 Ottawa/Gatineau B2B Mechanic Powertrain & Front-Clip Sheets** with zero Solera Hollander dependency, and EXIF-stripped Facebook Marketplace & Kijiji payloads with FB/Kijiji-only `"Ottawa South / Hwy 416 Corridor"` `RULE-MKT-002` pickup masking), and **Ontario Domestic CAD Checkout (`FR-CHK-01` / `DOMESTIC_ORIGIN`)** with **Channel-Split Ontario 13% HST & eBay Canada Billing Agent Accounting (`RULE-TAX-HST-01`: `0` corporate HST liability on eBay Canada sales + 13% seller-fee ITC vs. direct 13% HST trust ring-fencing)**, `"Delist on eBay First"` anti-double-sale sync, independent `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` Operator Float (`HELD_IN_OPERATOR_FLOAT`) subledger, `ITA s. 78(1)` deferred lease accruals, and **3-Tranche `$44,000 CAD` Senior Secured Loan (`$22k / $12k / $10k`)** with **1-Tap `cfoOverride`** on all capital/deal gates.
   * **Ramsayville Retail, Shop & Fulfillment Concept (`RAMS` — `ZONE_RAMSAYVILLE_SHOP`, Salvage-Only, `locationOperationalStatus = 'PROPOSED_INACTIVE'`):** Salvage-exclusive commercial shop, DriveON 10-day re-inspection & deposit-triggered 36-day SSC retail beater flip (`RETAIL_LOT_VEHICLE_SALE`), and concrete bay/lot concept belonging **exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`**. Seeded (`locationOperationalStatus = 'PROPOSED_INACTIVE'`, `maxActiveAssetCapacity = 8`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling = true`) for concrete bay/lot routing tests—emitting `PROPOSED_INACTIVE_LOCATION_WARNING` in `"WARN_ONLY"` mode (`422` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`) until Ottawa zoning and licensing are verified.

Zero-consignment outbound export pallet/container manifesting (`FR-LOG-01`/`02`) is gated on 100% upfront CAD/USD settlement in `SETTLED_ORIGIN_CORP_BANK` (`exportFulfillmentMode = 'PREPAID_ORIGIN_ONLY'`), LHD-to-RHD destination fitment guards, and broker-ready Commercial Invoice & Packing List generation, while the Trinidad Bonded Vault COD subsystem (`POSV`) remains schema-ready and dormant behind `IPolicyConfigPort`.

## Multi-Schema PostgreSQL Isolation, Supavisor-Safe `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` & SaaS Portability

The PostgreSQL database is partitioned into three schemas via Prisma `previewFeatures = ["multiSchema"]`—where `shared` is strictly a **technical multi-tenant table grouping** (not evidence of shared corporate finances, combined inventory, or shared facilities). Because Prisma connects directly over TCP rather than PostgREST (`auth.jwt()` evaluates to `NULL` and default `postgres` table-owner connections bypass RLS), runtime isolation is enforced at the database engine layer ([`ADR-0005`](./docs/adr/0005-rls-pooler-safety-ebay-billing-agent-and-post2025-cannabis.md)):

* **Dedicated `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS`) & `withTenantTransaction`:** Application queries connect over Supavisor transaction pooling (`?pgbouncer=true` on port `6543`; `DIRECT_URL` on port `5432` for migrations) as dedicated role `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS`, `plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan`). Every repository operation executes inside `withTenantTransaction(prisma, ctx, fn)`, which opens an interactive `prisma.$transaction` and sets transaction-local `set_config('app.current_org_id', ..., true)` (`is_local = true`). Every tenant-scoped table across `shared`, `salvage`, and `cannabis` enforces `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` evaluating `"organizationId" = shared.current_org_id()` (failing closed on `NULL`) plus composite tenant foreign keys (`@@unique([organizationId, id])`).
* **`shared` schema (`@@schema("shared")`):** `Organization`, `User` (authenticated via `@supabase/ssr` `await supabase.auth.getUser()`), `OrganizationMembership`, `StorageLocation` (with `organizationId`, `allowedOrgType`, `locationOperationalStatus`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling`, and `maxActiveAssetCapacity`), `CustodyEvent`, `Expense` (with `itcReceivableCadMinor` & `isDeferredIntercompanyAccrual`), `CashCustodyTransfer`, `OutboxEvent`, and `AuditLog`.
* **`salvage` schema (`@@schema("salvage")`):** `Vehicle` (HTA s. 60 register & DriveON timers), `Part` (`RESTOCKED_QC_HOLD`, `CSV_EXPORTED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION`, `tdgHazmatCode`), `ScrapComponentLog`, `Shipment`, `ShipmentPallet`, `PreOrderWishList`, `B2BReservation`, `SaleTransaction` (channel-split `RULE-TAX-HST-01` with `buyerTaxRemittedByPlatformMinor`, `itcReceivableCadMinor`, & `ebayDelistAttestedAt`), `SaleTransactionPartItem`, `SaleTransactionVehicleItem`, `CycleCountTask`, and `CurrencySettlement`—all owned independently by `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`.
* **`cannabis` schema (`@@schema("cannabis")` — Dormant DDL in Phase 1A):** `CannabisStrain`, `CannabisLot`, `ProductionRun`, `ProductionRunInput`, `ProductionRunOutput`, `CannabisDestructionLog` (3-stream), and `CanonicalInventoryEvent`—all owned independently by `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`.
* **Commercial Vertical-SaaS Portability (`IPolicyConfigPort`):** All market nodes (`DOMESTIC_ORIGIN`, `DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT`), sales channels (`LOCAL_WALK_IN_CASH`, `RETAIL_LOT_VEHICLE_SALE`, `DESTINATION_VAULT_COD`), cash custody states (`HELD_IN_OPERATOR_FLOAT`, `HELD_IN_DESTINATION_VAULT`, `SETTLED_ORIGIN_CORP_BANK`), and location zoning/capacity rules are data-driven and resolved per organization via `IPolicyConfigPort` (`src/core/config/pricing-policy.json` with optional `Organization.policyConfigJson` overrides).

> **Audit & Entity Isolation Guarantee:** `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (Kemptville) and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (Brockville) share zero inventory, cash balances, R2 object prefixes, or physical facilities. A Health Canada CTLS inspector or CRA Excise auditor reviewing `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` has **zero visibility** into `salvage` schema tables, export manifests, or Ontario automotive cash sales. Simultaneously, composite tenant foreign keys and data-driven location isolation (`RULE-ZONE-001` via `StorageLocation.organizationId` and `StorageLocation.allowedOrgType`) hard-block scanning or linking any `salvage` asset/part into a `cannabis` facility location or vice versa (`422 SITE_ZONING_VIOLATION`).

## Modular Directory Structure

```text
src/
├── core/
│   ├── domain/                    # Pure TypeScript domain calculators & 5-axis state guards
│   ├── ports/                     # Hexagonal Port Interfaces (IPolicyConfigPort, IStoragePort, ILabelTagPort, etc.)
│   ├── events/                    # Domain Event contracts & Outbox publisher
│   └── config/                    # Versioned regulatory, tariff, FX & mass-balance parameters
├── modules/
│   ├── shared-ops/                # 8.5"x11" synthetic laser QR tag sheets (+ ZPL), camera scanner,
│   │                              # hierarchical StorageLocation, receipt-gated Expense & float ledger, R2 adapters
│   ├── dismantling/               # Salvage Context 1: Asset intake, stripping, bin custody, return QC hold
│   ├── marketplace/               # Salvage Context 2: Two-step eBay CSV/API sync, B2B storefront, WhatsApp
│   ├── logistics-vault/           # Salvage Context 3: Pallets, manifests, Domestic & Trinidad COD Vault checkout
│   ├── cannabis-compliance/       # Dormant Phase 2B Cannabis Context: Propagation, M:N ProductionRun,
│   │                              # tenth-gram CanonicalInventoryEvent, 3-stream destruction, CTLS & CRA B300
│   └── cfo-subledger/             # Financial Context: Capital gate, expenses/ITCs, HST ring-fencing, FX, GL sync
├── infrastructure/
│   ├── db/                        # Prisma multi-schema client, withTenantTransaction, migrations, erp_app_user RLS
│   ├── adapters/                  # Concrete implementations of core/ports
│   └── workers/                   # BullMQ worker entrypoints
├── app/                           # Next.js 15 App Router (Workspace Switcher, UI, Server Actions)
└── extensions/
    └── local-marketplace-lister/  # Manifest V3 Chrome Extension for 1-click FB Marketplace & Kijiji autofill
```
