# Canada-First Ontario Domestic Pivot & Dormant Bonded Export Vault Gate

With neither a bonded family storage facility nor dedicated on-the-ground vault administration currently active in Trinidad, Phase 1 pivots `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` from speculative ocean-freight consignment (`DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT` / `DESTINATION_VAULT_COD`) to a 100% Canada-based operating model centered on Kemptville container shelving (`KEMP-C01`–`C04`), Zero-Typing eBay Motors & stealth Ottawa classifieds syndication (`FR-MKT-00`, `RULE-MKT-001`, `RULE-MKT-002`), Ramsayville retail vehicle flips & CAD checkout (`FR-CHK-01`, `DOMESTIC_ORIGIN`), and strictly 100% pre-paid origin-settled direct exports (`RULE-EXP-001`). Rather than stripping `DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT` (`POSV`), `CycleCountTask`, `CurrencySettlement`, and TTD landed-cost fields out of `prisma/schema.prisma`, we intentionally keep the multi-node export schema intact and gate it behind `IPolicyConfigPort` (`exportPolicy.exportFulfillmentMode = 'PREPAID_ORIGIN_ONLY'`) while pulling automated eBay Inventory API sync forward into Phase 2A. This avoids destructive multi-schema migrations when an overseas bonded facility or external Vertical-SaaS export tenant comes online later, while eliminating Day-1 Caribbean credit, shrinkage, and FX-repatriation risk.
