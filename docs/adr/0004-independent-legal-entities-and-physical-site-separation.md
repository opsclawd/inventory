# Independent Legal Entities, Separate Physical Facilities & Shared-Software-Only Boundary

Status: Accepted (Amends physical-site and entity-boundary assumptions in `ADR-0001`, `ADR-0002`, and `ADR-0003`)

Earlier specification drafts erroneously described `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` as partner-co-located ventures operating on "80% shared physical warehouse infrastructure" across a single 19-acre Kemptville parcel (`ZONE_GREENHOUSE_AG` / `ZONE_TREE_FARM` alongside `ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD`), and treated Ramsayville (`RAMS`) as an already-active concrete teardown facility. In reality, the platform serves **two independent legal corporations operating at completely separate physical facilities**: (1) **`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`salvage` schema)** operates its primary physical salvage, dismantling, parts warehousing, syndication, and domestic checkout business at **Kemptville, Ontario (`KEMP`)**—with **Ramsayville (`RAMS`)** preserved strictly as a salvage-only shop/retail/fulfillment concept whose operational readiness remains subject to verification, and **Port of Spain (`POSV`)** retained as a dormant future salvage-export vault; and (2) **`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (`cannabis` schema)** conducts all planned licensed cannabis cultivation, genetics/propagation, processing, lot genealogy, and regulatory reporting exclusively at **Brockville, Ontario (`BROCK`)**, subject to its actual Health Canada licence (with **zero** cannabis facilities, greenhouses, or inventory at Kemptville).

We retain the single modular monolith codebase, three-schema PostgreSQL layout (`shared`, `salvage`, `cannabis`), and generic `shared-ops` software components (`ILabelTagPort`, camera QR scanner, data-driven `StorageLocation` attributes [`organizationId`, `allowedOrgType`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling`, `maxActiveAssetCapacity`], `CustodyEvent`, `Expense`, `CashCustodyTransfer`, `IStoragePort`, `OutboxEvent`, and `AuditLog`) strictly as **reusable multi-tenant software infrastructure**—never as evidence of shared physical facilities, shared label/printer hardware, combined inventory, pooled cash/working capital, or identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders. Cross-tenant data access is prohibited by default via mandatory `organizationId` ownership and Row-Level Security across all three PostgreSQL schemas; Phase 1A seeds `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`legalEntityName = "Ontario Salvage OpCo Inc."`, `isOntarioHstRegistered = true`, `isOmvicLicensed = false`, `publicPickupCityLabel = "Ottawa South / Hwy 416 Corridor"`) with `KEMP_ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD` (`maxActiveAssetCapacity = 4`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling = false` under `complianceEnforcementMode = "WARN_ONLY"`) as the primary salvage site alongside `RAMS` (`maxActiveAssetCapacity = 8`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling = true`) for concrete routing tests, seeds `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (`legalEntityName = "Brockville Cannabis Operations Inc."`) minimally with `BROCK_SITE_FACILITY` and `BROCK-PROP-R1-T2` strictly to test cross-tenant RLS denial and `RULE-ZONE-001` (`422 SITE_ZONING_VIOLATION`), keeps `isDeferredIntercompanyAccrual` generic with zero hardcoded vendor names, and seeds disjoint single-org users by default with only `ARCHITECT_CFO` granted dual `OrganizationMembership` rows to test Workspace Switcher visibility.
