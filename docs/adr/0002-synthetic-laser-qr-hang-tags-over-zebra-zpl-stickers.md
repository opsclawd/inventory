# Matte 10-MIL Synthetic Laser QR Hang-Tag Sheets Over Zebra Thermal Stickers

In unheated $-30^\circ\text{C}$ Kemptville 40ft storage containers (`KEMP-C01`–`C04`), direct-thermal labels blacken on contact with heat, motor oil, or brake cleaner, while industrial thermal-transfer adhesive stickers (`4" × 2"` Zebra `ZD421t`) fail to bond below $+10^\circ\text{C}$, curl off small curved castings and wiring harnesses (sensors, alternators, calipers, marine components), and leave adhesive residue on painted body panels. We therefore standardize Day-1 physical inventory and bin labelling across both `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (`RULE-TAG-001`) on **matte `10-MIL` (`120 lb` cover) waterproof synthetic laser sheets (`8.5" × 11"` Letter, e.g., TerraSlate)** printed with fused laser toner in **`3×4` (12-up)** or **`4×4` (16-up)** grids (`300–400` tags per 25-sheet pack), punched with a **$15\text{mm} \times 4\text{mm}$ oval slot** (or dual top holes for steel rack upright bin cards `KEMP-C01-*`), and fastened via **$-40^\circ\text{C}$-rated black UV-stabilized Nylon 6/6 zip ties**. In `src/modules/shared-ops/locations-qr`, `ILabelTagPort` generates deterministic in-memory `8.5" × 11"` PDF and SVG tag sheets ($\ge 1.5" \times 1.5"$ high-contrast QR codes with $\ge 4$-module quiet zones for glare-free dark-container `@zxing/browser` scanning) as the primary output while retaining Zebra ZPL II string generation as a secondary adapter format for Vertical-SaaS portability and future $4" \times 6"$ outbound parcel shipping labels.
