# Prisma/Supavisor Transaction-Local RLS Hardening, eBay GST/HST Billing Agent Split & Post-2025 Cannabis DDL

Status: Accepted (Refines database RLS, marketplace tax/compliance, and dormant Phase 2B cannabis DDL baselines established in `ADR-0001`–`ADR-0004`; specific `shared.User` RLS, `OrganizationMembership.isActive`, `tx.$queryRaw` transaction wrapper, settlement-liquidity separation, jurisdiction-aware GST/HST, trigger-based `ProductionRun` finalization, and fail-closed CI verification clauses amended by [`ADR-0006`](./0006-identity-rls-settlement-liquidity-jurisdiction-tax-and-run-finalization.md))

Following five technical and statutory verification audits across PostgreSQL/Supavisor multi-tenancy, CRA GST/HST marketplace tax law, eBay Motors Canada syndication policies, Ontario `O. Reg. 85/16` / TDG dangerous-goods shipping rules, and the March/April 2025 federal *Cannabis Regulations* amendments (`SOR/2025-43` and `SOR/2025-45`), we adopt three hardening refinements while preserving our 10-issue Phase 1A execution backlog and `ADR-0003` `"WARN_ONLY"` physical-custody agility:

1. **Supavisor-Safe Transaction-Local RLS, `erp_app_user` & Composite Tenant Foreign Keys (`shared`, `salvage`, `cannabis`):** Because Prisma connects directly to PostgreSQL over TCP rather than PostgREST, `auth.jwt()` evaluates to `NULL` and default `postgres` table-owner connections bypass Row-Level Security. We provision a dedicated least-privilege runtime role `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS`, `plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan`) for Supavisor transaction pooling (`?pgbouncer=true` on port `6543`, keeping `DIRECT_URL` on port `5432` for DDL migrations), apply `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` across every tenant-scoped table in `shared`, `salvage`, and `cannabis` evaluating `"organizationId" = shared.current_org_id()` (failing closed on `NULL`), enforce `@@unique([organizationId, id])` and composite intra-tenant foreign keys (`fields: [organizationId, <fk>Id], references: [organizationId, id]`) so `REFERENCES` checks can never cross tenant boundaries, ban session-level `SET` and autocommit `set_config` in favor of mandatory `withTenantTransaction(prisma, ctx, fn)` wrappers executing transaction-local `set_config('app.current_org_id' | 'app.current_user_id' | 'app.current_role', ..., true)`, and mandate `@supabase/ssr` `await supabase.auth.getUser()` (banning server-side `getSession()`), awaited Next.js 15 `cookies()`/`headers()`, real PostgreSQL RLS integration tests running as `erp_app_user`, and a CI no-stub composition gate (`! grep -rnE "not wired|TODO.*compose" src/ app/`).
2. **Channel-Split Ontario HST (`RULE-TAX-HST-01`), eBay Ship-From Truth (`RULE-MKT-002`), Two-Step CSV Sync, Return QC Hold, TDG Hazmat Guard & EASR Threshold:** Under Canada's Marketplace Facilitator / GST/HST Billing Agent rules, eBay collects and remits 100% of buyer GST/HST on `EBAY_MOTORS_SHIPPED` (`EBAY_CA`) directly to the CRA—so `SaleTransaction` records `buyerTaxRemittedByPlatformMinor` for audit visibility, adds `0` to corporate `taxOrVatMinor` (`hstCollectedTrustLiabilityMinor`), and records the 13% HST charged by eBay *on eBay seller fees* as recoverable Input Tax Credit (`itcReceivableCadMinor`), whereas direct/classified sales collect 13% HST and ring-fence `netHstTrustLiability = max(0, hstCollectedDirect - verifiedItcReceivable)` out of `AvailableWorkingCapital_CAD` and `DistributableRealizedProfitPool_CAD`. To satisfy carrier rate tables and eBay Item Location policies, `EBAY_CA` exports always declare the true warehouse location (`Kemptville, ON, K0G 1J0`), restricting `"Ottawa South / Hwy 416 Corridor"` masking strictly to Facebook Marketplace and Kijiji ad payloads while gating `RAMS` behind `locationOperationalStatus = "PROPOSED_INACTIVE"`. In addition, Seller Hub CSV exports (with parent/child `Relationship=Compatibility` rows) transition `Part.ebaySyncState` to `CSV_EXPORTED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION` until an `ebayListingId` confirms `SYNDICATED_LIVE` (paired with a local POS "Delist on eBay First" deep-link attestation), returned parts enter `PartCustodyState.RESTOCKED_QC_HOLD` to block part-swap auto-relisting, `Part.tdgHazmatCode` (`NONE | UN3268_AIRBAG | UN0503_PYROTECHNIC | UN2794_WET_BATTERY | UN3480_LITHIUM_BATTERY`) hard-blocks hazmat parcel shipping on eBay, and `easrRegistrationNumber: null` appends `MISSING_EASR_REGISTRATION_WARNING` in `"WARN_ONLY"` mode on vehicle `#3+` without blocking intake.
3. **Post-2025 Federal Cannabis Rules & Dormant M:N `ProductionRun` + `CanonicalInventoryEvent` DDL (Zero Phase 1A Cannabis Code):** We align dormant Phase 2B Brockville specifications with `SOR/2025-43` and `SOR/2025-45` (`800 m²` micro-cultivation canopy under s. 13(1)(a); `2,400 kg` annual micro-processing limit plus the s. 21(3) unlimited exemption when 100% of non-plant/seed cannabis is grown on-site; dried/fresh NNCP exemption vs. 60-day extract NNCP, s. 88.94 Preventive Control Plan, and s. 101.3 `<= 1,000 mg` Total THC non-discrete container cap; 3-stream destruction across `CULTIVATION_WASTE` [0 witnesses, 0 s. 229 records, 0 CTLS], `WHOLE_PLANTS` [1 employee witness + `plantCount` only], and `OTHER_CANNABIS` [1 employee witness + pre-destruction net weight, optional 2nd witness toggle for excise-stamped goods]; and `7-year` general / `25-year` s. 248 adverse-reaction retention) and replace the 1:1 `CannabisLotTransformation` table in dormant `cannabis` DDL with M:N `ProductionRun` (`ProductionRunInput` / `ProductionRunOutput`) + append-only `CanonicalInventoryEvent` (`BigInt` `netMassTenthGrams`, `flowerMassTenthGrams`, `nonFlowerMassTenthGrams`, `discreteUnits`, `totalThcMg`), keeping all cannabis application code, UI, and reporting engines deferred to Phase 2B.
