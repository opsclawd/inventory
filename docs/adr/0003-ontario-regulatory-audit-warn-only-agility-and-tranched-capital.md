> **Scope Correction (`ADR-0004`):** Historical decision rationale preserved. Following the Eastern Ontario regulatory and working-capital feasibility audit, we adopt six operational and statutory safeguards while preserving multi-tenant `cannabis` and dormant `POSV` schemas as zero-cost Vertical-SaaS optionality: (1) **Zero-Fluid Farm Split (`O. Reg. 85/16` / `RULE-ENV-001`)** moving fluid drainage and vehicle teardown to Ramsayville concrete (`RAMS`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling = true`) while keeping Kemptville containers (`KEMP-C01`–`C04`, `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling = false`) as a dry clean-parts warehouse; (2) **Ontario HTA Section 60 Garage & Wrecking Register** provenance fields on `Vehicle`; (3) **5-Stream Commodity Scrap Ledger (`ScrapComponentLog`)** with catalytic converter disposition tracking; (4) **DriveON 10-Day Re-Inspection & Deposit-Triggered 36-Day SSC Timers**; (5) **3-Tranche `$44,000 CAD` Senior Secured Loan (`$22k / $12k / $10k` above the `$7,500` reserve floor)**, `ITA s. 78(1)` deferred inter-company rent accrual (`Expense.isDeferredIntercompanyAccrual`), `$3,200` landed donor SUV cap, and Year-1 `HIGH_END_REBUILD` pause—all supporting an audited **1-Tap `cfoOverride`**; and (6) **Ontario 13% HST trust ring-fencing** (excluding `SaleTransaction.taxOrVatMinor` from `netRealizedCadMinor` and profit draws) plus a **Day-1 Ottawa/Gatineau B2B Mechanic Powertrain Sheet** (using NHTSA vPIC + eBay Compatibility APIs rather than Solera Hollander).

Crucially, to guarantee that software friction never causes PostgreSQL to diverge from real-world physical location truth during cold-start yard operations, regulatory/documentation checks (1)–(4) are governed by an organization-scoped policy flag `operationalGates.complianceEnforcementMode: "WARN_ONLY" | "STRICT_BLOCK"` defaulting to **`"WARN_ONLY"`** in Phase 1A. In `"WARN_ONLY"` mode, scans and lifecycle transitions on items (1)–(4) never throw a blocking HTTP `422`; instead, they commit the physical custody mutation and append structured warning codes (`COMPLIANCE_WARNING_UNDRAINED_FARM`, `MISSING_HTA_S60_DOCS`, `UNRECONCILED_CATALYTIC_CONVERTER`, `DRIVEON_SAFETY_CERT_WARNING`) to `complianceWarnings` for asynchronous CFO review, whereas cross-tenant `allowedOrgType` isolation, 3-tap QR bin scans, `HELD_IN_OPERATOR_FLOAT` cash tracking, 13% HST exclusion, and 1-tap-overridable capital/deal gates remain strictly enforced.
