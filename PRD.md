# PRD.md — Functional Specification & Phased Execution Backlog

## 1. Product Scope & Operational Mandate

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the functional workflows, role permissions, acceptance criteria, and phased delivery milestones for the **Unified Multi-Tenant Modular Monolith**, a reusable operations and compliance ERP platform serving **two independent legal corporations operating at completely separate physical facilities** ([`ADR-0004`](./docs/adr/0004-independent-legal-entities-and-physical-site-separation.md)). The corporations share **reusable software infrastructure only** (`shared` DB schema & `src/modules/shared-ops` — `8.5" × 11"` 10-MIL synthetic laser QR hang-tag sheet & optional ZPL generation, mobile camera barcode scanning, generic hierarchical `StorageLocation` custody, organization-scoped receipt-gated `Expense` & cash-custody abstractions, and tenant-segregated Cloudflare R2 storage), with **no shared physical warehouse, combined inventory, shared bank accounts or cash floats, shared operating permits, or assumed identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders**:

1. **Business A — Automotive & Marine Salvage (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — `salvage` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity):**
   * **Primary Physical Operating Location:** **Kemptville, Ontario (`KEMP` — `ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD`)** — vehicle & watercraft acquisition, dismantling, HTA Section 60 statutory register, 5-stream commodity scrap (`ScrapComponentLog`), 40ft container parts warehousing (`C01`–`C04`), 3-tap QR hang-tag custody, eBay Motors / B2B Mechanic Powertrain Sheets / Local Classifieds syndication, and Ontario Domestic CAD Checkout (`DOMESTIC_ORIGIN`).
   * **Ramsayville Retail, Shop & Fulfillment Concept (`RAMS` — `ZONE_RAMSAYVILLE_SHOP`, Salvage-Only, Subject to Verification):** Salvage-exclusive shop, quick-turn retail beater flip (`RETAIL_LOT_VEHICLE_SALE`), and East Ottawa customer pickup concept belonging **exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`** (architectural distinction preserved via configurable `StorageLocation` flags; operational status remains subject to confirmation).
   * **Pre-Paid Direct Export & Dormant Destination Bonded Vault (`POSV` — `DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT`):** 100% Pre-Paid Direct Export (`RULE-EXP-001`), with the Trinidad destination vault (`POSV`) retained as a dormant/future salvage-export capability until Phase 3 activation.
2. **Business B — Cannabis Cultivation & Processing (`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` — `cannabis` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity):**
   * **Exclusive Physical Operating Location:** **Brockville, Ontario (`BROCK` — `ZONE_BROCKVILLE_FACILITY`)** — licensed cannabis cultivation, genetics and propagation (mother plants, clone trays), cannabis processing ("moving the juice" / extraction & bulk lot transformation), lot/batch genealogy, inventory custody, QAP quality assurance, and Health Canada CTLS / CRA B300 regulatory reporting, subject to the corporation's actual Health Canada licence and authorized activities. *(Kemptville is strictly a salvage site and has zero cannabis nursery, greenhouse, cultivation, or processing operations.)*

## 2. Sanitized Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix, Identity RLS & Workspace Switcher

Authentication is enforced via `@supabase/ssr` using **`await supabase.auth.getUser()`** (`getSession()` is strictly banned in server code per `ADR-0005` & `ADR-0006`) and awaited Next.js 15 `await cookies()` / `await headers()`, paired with dedicated least-privilege `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS`, `plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan`) connections, `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` across all tables in `shared` (**including `shared.User`**), `salvage`, and `cannabis`, and parameterized `tx.$queryRaw` transaction-local tenant binding via `withTenantTransaction(prisma, ctx, fn)`. Runtime permissions derive strictly from the authenticated user's explicit active **`OrganizationMembership` (`organizationId`, `role`, `isActive == true`)** for the active organization—never from `User.role` (a non-authoritative onboarding display field) or client-supplied org/role parameters (`ADR-0004` & `ADR-0006`):
* **Membership Activation, Revocation & Reactivation (`OrganizationMembership.isActive` — `FIX 01`):** Memberships are created active (`isActive = true`) by `ARCHITECT_CFO` in that organization (or seed fixtures). Deactivating/revoking a membership (`isActive = false`, stamping `deactivatedAt`, `deactivatedById`, and `revocationReason` while preserving the row for historical audit/financial FKs) **immediately** terminates access to that organization at both `auth-context.ts` (`403 Forbidden`) and PostgreSQL `shared.current_org_id()` (`NULL` fail-closed RLS). Only `ARCHITECT_CFO` of that organization can reactivate a deactivated membership (`isActive = true`, `deactivatedAt = null`).
* **Global Identity Firewall (`shared.User` — `FIX 02`):** Under `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` (`user_select_self_or_org_comember` + column-restricted self-profile `UPDATE`), users in `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` can read only themselves and members of `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`, and can never enumerate or mutate unrelated `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` personnel (and vice versa).
* **MFA Assurance (`aal2`):** Users holding `ARCHITECT_CFO`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, or `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE` memberships MUST have Multi-Factor Authentication (`mfaEnrolled = true`) enabled, and privileged financial, compliance, or gate-release mutations verify active session assurance (`aal2`), not merely the `User.mfaEnrolled` boolean.

### 2.1 Organization Workspace Switcher & Multi-Tenant View Isolation

* **Default Single-Organization Isolation (Zero Assumed Cross-Entity Access):**
  * **Salvage-Scoped Memberships (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — e.g., `ARCHITECT_CFO`, `HUNTER_MECHANIC`, `ONTARIO_STRIPPER`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, `OVERSEAS_B2B_BUYER`):** Bound exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`. Users only see Salvage/Vault navigation, Kemptville primary locations (`KEMP-C01..C04`), proposed salvage-only `RAMS` (`PROPOSED_INACTIVE`) / dormant `POSV` (`DORMANT_FUTURE`) locations, and `salvage` schema views; they have zero visibility into `cannabis` tables or Brockville records, and the Workspace Switcher is hidden.
  * **Cannabis-Scoped Memberships (`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` — e.g., `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`, `NURSERY_CULTIVATION_TECH`, or a cannabis-entity `ARCHITECT_CFO`):** Bound exclusively to `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`. Users only see Brockville Cannabis Cultivation & Processing navigation, Brockville facility locations (`BROCK-*`), and `cannabis` schema views; they have zero visibility into `salvage` tables, Kemptville locations, or automotive cash sales, and the Workspace Switcher is hidden.
* **Explicit Multi-Organization Memberships (Never Inferred by Default):**
  * The platform **never** infers identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders across `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`legalEntityName = "Ontario Salvage OpCo Inc."`) and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (`legalEntityName = "Brockville Cannabis Operations Inc."`).
  * **If and only if** an authenticated user has been explicitly granted active `OrganizationMembership` rows (`isActive == true`) in more than one `Organization` (in Phase 1A seed data, **disjoint single-org users are seeded by default** across `HUNTER_MECHANIC`, `ONTARIO_STRIPPER`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, `OVERSEAS_B2B_BUYER` in `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` only and `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`, `NURSERY_CULTIVATION_TECH` in `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` only, plus a deactivated membership fixture `isActive = false` for negative revocation testing, with **only `ARCHITECT_CFO`** granted active memberships in both organizations to test switcher visibility), the header renders the **Organization Workspace Switcher** (`[Auto & Marine Salvage]` $\leftrightarrow$ `[Cannabis Cultivation & Processing]`). Switching workspaces validates an active `OrganizationMembership` (`isActive == true`) in the target organization and updates the server-verified active organization context passed into `withTenantTransaction` (`tx.$queryRaw` `set_config('app.current_org_id', orgId, true)`), scoping all shared tables (`User`, `StorageLocation`, `Expense`, `CashCustodyTransfer`, `CustodyEvent`, `OutboxEvent`, `AuditLog`) and domain queries strictly to the selected corporation.

### Module A (Phase 1A): Asset & Part Intake, HTA s. 60 Register, EASR/Return QC & Append-Only Custody (`src/modules/dismantling`)

#### FR-INT-01: Multi-Category Donor & Whole-Unit Intake, HTA Section 60 Register, `O. Reg. 85/16` EASR Warning & Capital/Deal Gates
* **Description:** The system shall support registering three distinct `AssetCategory` types: `AUTOMOBILE` (17-character VIN), `WATERCRAFT` (12-character Hull Identification Number / HIN), and `SHOP_EQUIPMENT` (Manufacturer Serial Number) inside `withTenantTransaction`, maintaining a statutory Ontario *Highway Traffic Act* Section 60 Garage & Wrecking Register (`?view=hta-s60-register`) while enforcing capital/deal discipline gates with a 1-tap `cfoOverride`.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. When `assetCategory == 'AUTOMOBILE'`, the system calls `IAssetDecoderPort` (NHTSA vPIC) to auto-populate Year, Make, Model, Trim, and Engine, and defaults `steeringSide = 'LHD_ONLY'` for North American acquisitions.
  2. When `assetCategory` is `'WATERCRAFT'` or `'SHOP_EQUIPMENT'`, the system sets `roadBrand = 'NOT_APPLICABLE'`, `steeringSide = 'UNIVERSAL'`, and bypasses all Ontario HTA Section 60, `O. Reg. 85/16` ELV EASR, and DriveON road-inspection checks.
  3. **Ontario HTA Section 60 Statutory Register, `O. Reg. 85/16` EASR Threshold & `WARN_ONLY` Agility (`complianceEnforcementMode`):** Captures `acquisitionSource`, `sellerFullName`, `sellerAddress`, `sellerPhotoIdStorageKey` (`R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`), `ontarioPermitNumber` (green-slip permit #), and `declaredStatutoryPurpose`, plus optional DriveON 10-day re-inspection fail-sheet timestamps (`driveOnInitialFailAt`, `driveOnReinspectionDeadlineAt = +10 days`, `driveOnFailSheetStorageKey`). In `"WARN_ONLY"` mode (Phase 1A default):
     * Missing HTA s. 60 fields on an `AUTOMOBILE` **never block intake (`422`)**—the vehicle is created and `'MISSING_HTA_S60_DOCS'` is appended to `Vehicle.complianceWarnings` (`422` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
     * Intaking active `AUTOMOBILE` ELV `#3+` while `complianceThresholds.easrRegistrationNumber == null` (`O. Reg. 85/16` $> 2$ ELV threshold) appends `'MISSING_EASR_REGISTRATION_WARNING'` without blocking intake (`422 EASR_REGISTRATION_REQUIRED` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
     * Routing intake to a `StorageLocation` with `locationOperationalStatus == 'PROPOSED_INACTIVE'` (such as `RAMS`) appends `'PROPOSED_INACTIVE_LOCATION_WARNING'` without blocking intake (`422 LOCATION_PROPOSED_INACTIVE` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
  4. **Enforced Capital, Deal-Cap, Lane-Pause & Capacity Gates with 1-Tap `cfoOverride`:** Enforces Tranche 1 `workingCapitalGate` (`$22,000 CAD` active principal minus `netHstTrustLiability` and `$7,500 CAD` `minCashReserveFloorCadMinor` = `$14,500 CAD` launch buying power), `maxDonorDismantleLandedCadMinor` (`$3,200 CAD` cap on `DISMANTLE_AND_EXPORT`), `enableHighEndRebuildLane = false` (Year-1 pause on `HIGH_END_REBUILD`), and destination `StorageLocation.maxActiveAssetCapacity`. Every capital/deal/capacity gate supports a **1-Tap `cfoOverride`** (`cfoOverride: { approved: true, reasonNote }` by `ARCHITECT_CFO`, stamping `cfoGateApprovedById`, `cfoGateApprovedAt`, `cfoGateOverrideReason`, and `AuditLog`).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**

##### Pre-Bid Market Demand Validation & Sold-Comparable Evidence (Phase 1B)
* **Scope Note:** This subsection defines **Phase 1B** product requirements for evidence-based donor acquisition underwriting. Detailed technical architecture, mathematical underwriting formulas, external data integrations, and implementation tickets are deferred until Phase 1B planning and do not alter Phase 1A scope or Issues `#1`–`#10`.
* **Functional Requirements:**
  1. **Component-Level Demand Assessment:**
     * Before approving a donor vehicle acquisition, the system must support evaluating market demand for high-value recoverable components rather than relying solely on theoretical part values.
     * Initial target components include:
       * Engine Control Module (ECM/ECU).
       * ABS pump/control module.
       * Infotainment display/head unit.
       * Left and right headlights.
       * Left and right taillights.
     * The target component list must be configurable and extensible for other valuable assemblies, including transmissions, transfer cases, differentials, and marine components.
     * Component identification must account for OEM part numbers, compatible vehicles, trim, engine, generation, and relevant interchange restrictions.
  2. **Sold-Comparable Market Evidence:**
     * For each assessed component, the system must support recording:
       * Relevant sold-comparable prices, preferably from the preceding 90 days.
       * Number of comparable units sold.
       * Estimated 90-day sell-through rate, when reliably available.
       * Active competing listing count and pricing.
       * Evidence source and research timestamp.
       * Fitment and comparability confidence.
       * Insufficient or unavailable market data indicators.
     * The system must strictly differentiate actual completed sales from active asking prices and must never fabricate historical sales volumes or sell-through metrics from active listings alone.
  3. **Risk-Adjusted Acquisition Evaluation:**
     * The Deal Analyzer must evaluate:
       * Conservative component resale value.
       * Likelihood of recovering each component in saleable condition.
       * Expected time to sale.
       * Marketplace fees and selling costs.
       * Stripping, testing, and handling expenses.
       * Expected near-term cash recovery.
       * Expected longer-term inventory realization.
       * Scrap recovery as a downside consideration.
     * Acquisition decisions must prioritize cash recovery within the existing **14–21-day** operational target rather than theoretical total inventory value over 90 days, while maintaining full compatibility with existing `FR-DEAL-01` maximum allowable bid ($B_{\text{max}}$), working-capital reserve (`workingCapitalGate`), donor landed-cost cap (`dealDisciplineGate`), physical capacity, and `ARCHITECT_CFO` override (`FR-DEAL-02`) requirements. *(The exact mathematical underwriting formula is deferred to Phase 1B technical specification.)*
  4. **Configurable Demand Thresholds:**
     * A 90-day sell-through rate above `50%` may be used as a configurable positive demand signal; however, the system must:
       * Not require every evaluated component to exceed `50%`.
       * Not automatically reject attractive low-volume, high-margin components.
       * Account for sample size and evidence reliability.
       * Permit components with insufficient data to be assessed conservatively.
       * Allow audited `ARCHITECT_CFO` overrides (`cfoOverride`) where appropriate.
     * The final acquisition decision must be governed by overall risk-adjusted economics and liquidity constraints rather than a single arbitrary threshold.
  5. **Data Acquisition Strategy:**
     * Initial Phase 1B delivery may rely on manually researched and entered eBay Product Research (formerly Terapeak) observations.
     * Automated sold-comparable ingestion remains conditional upon legitimate data-provider access; the system must not assume that eBay's restricted `Marketplace Insights API` is publicly available to new developers.
     * eBay `Browse API` active-listing data may support competitor supply and asking-price analysis, but must never be treated as equivalent to completed-sale history.
     * The design must preserve the ability to incorporate authorized external data sources or internal realized sales history in later phases.
  6. **Acquisition Decision Evidence:**
     * The Deal Analyzer must retain sufficient structured evidence to explain why a donor acquisition was approved or rejected, including:
       * Vehicle identity.
       * Assessed components.
       * Market evidence and observation dates.
       * Estimated recoverable value.
       * Demand and uncertainty indicators.
       * Recommended maximum landed acquisition cost.
       * Approval, rejection, or override rationale, consistent with existing auditability requirements and `ARCHITECT_CFO` permissions.

#### FR-CAN-02: M:N Production Runs, Lifecycle State Machine & Tenth-Gram Dual CTLS/B300 Mass-Balance Ledger (`ProductionRun` & `CanonicalInventoryEvent`)
* **Description:** Operators recording wet-to-dry curing (`FRESH_HARVEST` $\rightarrow$ `DRIED_FLOWER` + `TRIM_BIOMASS`), solventless bubble-hash washing, live rosin pressing, or infused pre-roll blending shall create an M:N `ProductionRun` (`ProductionRunInput` $\times$ `ProductionRunOutput`) in `status = 'DRAFT'` or `'IN_PROGRESS'`, attach source and output lots in exact `BigInt` tenth-gram (`0.1 g` = `0.0001 kg`) integer units, and transition the run to `status = 'FINALIZED'` (`ADR-0006` `FIX 04`).
* **Business Rules:**
  1. **Explicit Production Run Lifecycle (`CannabisProductionRunStatus`):**
     * `DRAFT` $\rightarrow$ `IN_PROGRESS` $\rightarrow$ `FINALIZED` (or `DRAFT`/`IN_PROGRESS` $\rightarrow$ `VOIDED` with `voidReason` + `voidedAt`).
     * While `status IN ('DRAFT', 'IN_PROGRESS')`, operators may insert/update/delete child `ProductionRunInput` and `ProductionRunOutput` rows without premature row-level mass-balance `CHECK` failures.
     * `FINALIZED` and `VOIDED` runs are strictly immutable. Post-finalization errors are never corrected by mutating or deleting finalized rows; they require an auditable compensating `CanonicalInventoryEvent` (`reversesCanonicalEventId` + `reasonNote`) or a reversal `ProductionRun` (`reversesProductionRunId`).
  2. **Trigger-Enforced Finalization & Child Aggregate Reconciliation (`cannabis.enforce_production_run_lifecycle_and_finalization` + `cannabis.guard_production_run_child_mutation`):**
     * Every child `INSERT`/`UPDATE`/`DELETE` on `ProductionRunInput` or `ProductionRunOutput` acquires a transaction-scoped `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` row lock on the parent `ProductionRun` and rejects mutation unless parent `status IN ('DRAFT', 'IN_PROGRESS')`, serializing concurrent child edits against finalization.
     * Transitioning `ProductionRun.status` to `'FINALIZED'` verifies at the PostgreSQL trigger layer that: (a) $\ge 1$ `ProductionRunInput` row and $\ge 1$ `ProductionRunOutput` row exist for `(organizationId, productionRunId)`, (b) `totalInputMassTenthGrams == SUM(ProductionRunInput.consumedMassTenthGrams) > 0`, (c) `totalOutputMassTenthGrams == SUM(ProductionRunOutput.yieldedMassTenthGrams) > 0`, and (d) the canonical tenth-gram mass-balance equation holds:
       $$\text{TotalInputMass}_{0.1\text{g}} = \text{TotalOutputMass}_{0.1\text{g}} + \text{ProcessLossMass}_{0.1\text{g}} + \text{DestroyedByproductMass}_{0.1\text{g}} + \text{UnaccountedVariance}_{0.1\text{g}}$$
  3. **Internal 2.0% QAP Review Gate (`RULE-CAN-001` — Internal Quality Control, Never a Statutory Allowance):** Health Canada CTLS and CRA Form B300 require exact reconciliation of all unaccounted variance (`unaccountedVarianceTenthGrams`); **neither regulator grants a statutory "free 2% loss allowance."** When $|\text{UnaccountedVariance}_{0.1\text{g}}| \times 1000 > \text{TotalInputMass}_{0.1\text{g}} \times 20$ ($> 2.0\%$, or curing moisture loss falls outside the configurable `72.0%–80.0%` band), `requiresQapVarianceReview` must be `true`, and finalization requires either (i) all output `CannabisLot` rows placed on `complianceHold = true` pending QAP investigation or (ii) `verifiedByQapUserId IS NOT NULL` + non-empty `qapVarianceReasonNote`.
  4. **Micro-Processing `2,400 kg` Cap & `s. 21(3)` On-Site Grown Exemption (`SOR/2025-43`):** Tracks calendar-year dried-cannabis-equivalent intake against the **`2,400 kg/year`** Micro-Processing threshold (`s. 21(2)`), automatically applying the **`s. 21(3)` unlimited processing exemption** whenever $100\%$ of non-plant/seed cannabis handled on-site was grown under a co-located Micro-Cultivation licence at `BROCK`.

Cannabis extracts (bubble hash, live rosin, infused pre-rolls) enforce a **60-day `NNCP`** filing, a written **Preventive Control Plan (`PCP`, `s. 88.94`)**, **`<= 1,000 mg` Total THC** per non-discrete immediate container (`s. 101.3`), and a verified private Lab CoA PDF (`labCoaStorageKey` in `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`) while `complianceHold == false`.
  3. **Strict Audit Isolation:** CTLS and CRA B300 export queries execute inside `withTenantTransaction` as `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`) exclusively against `organizationId == ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`, guaranteeing zero visibility into `salvage.*` records.

  * Any manifested SKU not scanned into the vault within 24 hours of unstuffing flags a **Transit Shrinkage Exception** on the CFO dashboard.

#### FR-VLT-02: Landed Cost Allocation, TTD Floor Pricing & 3-Tier Shipment P&L
* **Description:** Upon entering the final customs broker assessment (`assessedDutyTtdMinor`, `assessedVatTtdMinor`, port drayage, and ocean freight) on the `Shipment`, the system allocates landed costs across all parts and whole units on the shipment (weighted by declared FOB value and weight), computes each item's minimum Cash-on-Delivery price (`vaultFloorPriceTtdMinor`), and tracks the shipment across the **3-Tier Shipment Profitability Model** (Tier 1 Projected Contribution CAD, Tier 2 Realized Operating Profit TTD/CAD, and Tier 3 Repatriated Cash Return CAD as defined in `DOMAIN-RULES.md` Section 4.4):
  $$\text{Floor}_{\text{TTD}} = \frac{\left(\text{CostBasis}_{\text{CAD}} \times (1 + \text{TargetMarkup}) \times \text{FX}_{\text{CAD\_to\_USD}} \times \text{FX}_{\text{shadow}}\right) + \text{AllocatedLandedTaxesAndFreight}_{\text{TTD}}}{1 - r_{\text{comm}}}$$
* **Business Rules:**
  * All tariff rates, VAT percentages ($12.5\%$), broker commission rates ($5\%\text{–}10\%$, calculated strictly on pre-tax `subtotalMinor` per `RULE-PRC-002`), and shadow FX reserve rates are read from the versioned configuration register (`DOMAIN-RULES.md`), never hardcoded in business logic.

#### FR-VLT-03: Strict COD Verification, 3-Tier Negotiation Corridor & Phased Gate Release
* **Description:** When a buyer or commissioned broker arrives at the Destination Vault to purchase parts or watercraft, the system enforces a hard separation between sales selection and payment verification while providing a bounded Vault Admin negotiation corridor and counter commission netting.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. Only `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN` or `ARCHITECT_CFO` with an active MFA-verified JWT session (`aal2`) can execute `verifyCodPayment` (`checkoutStrictnessMode == 'BONDED_VAULT_STRICT'` default for `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`, configurable to `'AUDIT_ENFORCED'` or `'STANDARD_POS'` for external SaaS yards).
  2. **3-Tier Price Floor Evaluation & Negotiation Corridor (`floorPriceAtCheckoutMinor`):**
     * **Tier 1 (At or Above Floor):** `grossCollectedMinor >= floorPriceAtCheckoutMinor` $\rightarrow$ Passes automatically (`discountReasonCode = null`).
     * **Tier 2 (Within Admin Negotiation Corridor):** Discount (`discountAmountMinor`) is $\le \min(\text{vaultAdminMaxDiscountCapTtdMinor } [\text{TT\$750}],\; \max(\text{vaultAdminSmallRoundingAllowanceTtdMinor } [\text{TT\$100}],\; \text{floorPriceAtCheckoutMinor} \times 0.08))$ $\rightarrow$ Allowed for `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN` (or `HUNTER_MECHANIC` in Ontario under `domesticAdminDiscountToleranceBps = 1000` [10%]) **if and only if** a valid `discountReasonCode` (`"CASH_ROUNDING" | "BUNDLE_DISCOUNT" | "TRANSIT_OR_SHOP_DAMAGE" | "AGED_INVENTORY_CLEARANCE"`) is selected.
     * **Tier 3 (Beyond Admin Corridor — Hard Lock / CFO Override Required):** Discount exceeds the Admin Corridor $\rightarrow$ Rejects with `422 FLOOR_PRICE_OVERRIDE_REQUIRED` unless `actorRole === 'ARCHITECT_CFO'` or a valid `cfoOverrideApprovedById` + `cfoOverrideReasonNote` (via CFO Override PIN/approval) is supplied.
  3. **Broker Commission Deducted at Counter Toggle (`isBrokerCommissionDeductedAtCounter`):** When `isBrokerCommissionDeductedAtCounter == true`, the broker retained their commission directly from the cash handed over at the counter, so the physical cash added to `HELD_IN_DESTINATION_VAULT` is `grossCollectedMinor - brokerCommissionMinor` (keeping physical vault safe cash 100% accurate).
  4. The admin must upload a payment proof photo (cash count or verified bank wire receipt) to `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`.
  5. **Trinidad FIU / CDD Compliance Gate (`RULE-COMP-FIU-01` — Encrypted Private ID Storage & 30-Day Rolling Window):** If a single transaction or linked buyer transactions total $\ge \text{TT\$50,000}$ (`5000000` minor units), the UI enforces uploading a photo/scan of the buyer's government ID (`buyerIdDocumentStorageKey` in `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`) and capturing the AES-256-GCM application-encrypted ID type and number (`buyerIdNumberEncrypted`). Linked transactions are evaluated deterministically across all non-voided `SaleTransaction` records (`marketNode == 'DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT'` and `isVoidedOrReversed == false`) within a **rolling 30-calendar-day window (`createdAt >= now() - 30 days`)** matching the same `buyerPhoneE164`, normalized `lower(trim(buyerName))`, or `buyerIdNumberEncrypted` (triggering the Government ID gate at $\ge \text{TT\$50,000}$ and the mandatory Large Cash Transaction Alert at $\ge \text{TT\$90,000}$).
  6. **Atomic Release vs. Two-Step Gate Token:** While counter and exit are operated by the same team, payment verification atomically stamps `gateReleasedAt = now()`, transitioning child parts to `custodyState = 'SOLD_AND_RELEASED'` (and whole assets to `lifecycleState = 'SOLD_EXPORT_COD'`). When dedicated exit guards operate separately from counter staff, verification issues a **Single-Use HMAC-SHA256 Gate Release QR Token** (`gateTokenNonce`, valid for 4 hours) consumed at the warehouse exit (returning HTTP `410 Gone` on replay).

#### FR-VLT-04: Commercial Bank Cap, Cash Custody Transfers & FX Repatriation Batch Ledger
* **Description:** Tracks TTD cash deposited from the Vault safe (`HELD_IN_DESTINATION_VAULT`) into Trinidadian commercial banks (`DEPOSITED_DESTINATION_BANK`) via `CashCustodyTransfer` and logs conversion batches (`CurrencySettlement`) back into CAD/USD (`SETTLED_ORIGIN_CORP_BANK`).

#### FR-VLT-05: Weekly Blind Cycle Counts & Bank Deposit Reconciliation
* **Description:** Once per week (or triggered on demand by `ARCHITECT_CFO`), generates a `CycleCountTask` batch selecting up to 10 random `SHELVED_DESTINATION_VAULT` parts/vehicles without revealing the expected shelf bin prior to arrival.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) & Quality Gates

* **NFR-SEC-01 (Tenant-Prefixed Storage Isolation):** `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET` shall block all public access, and all object keys across both `R2_PUBLIC_MEDIA_BUCKET` and `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET` shall be namespaced by `{organizationId}/...`. Receipts, UVIPs, bank deposit slips (`settlementProofStorageKey`), export bills of lading (`exportProofStorageKey`), marketplace tax invoices (`feeTaxInvoiceStorageKey`), encrypted customer government ID documents (`buyerIdDocumentStorageKey`), Lab CoA PDFs (`labCoaStorageKey`), and cannabis destruction evidence photos (`evidencePhotoStorageKey`) may only be accessed via server-generated pre-signed URLs (maximum TTL 15 minutes) after verifying an active `OrganizationMembership` (`isActive == true`) for that `organizationId`, logged in `AuditLog`.
* **NFR-SEC-02 (Supavisor-Safe `erp_app_user` `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, `shared.User` Identity Firewall & Composite Tenant FK Firewall [`ADR-0005` & `ADR-0006`]):** Because Prisma connects directly over TCP rather than PostgREST, `auth.jwt()` RLS policies are banned. Application queries run under dedicated least-privilege runtime role `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS`, `plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan`) over Supavisor transaction pooling (`?pgbouncer=true` on port `6543`) inside `withTenantTransaction(prisma, ctx, fn)`, which executes parameterized `tx.$queryRaw` `SELECT set_config(..., true)` (`is_local = true`) and verifies active membership via fixed-`search_path` (`SET search_path = ''`) `SECURITY DEFINER` helper `shared.current_org_id()`. Every table across `shared` (including `shared.User`), `salvage`, and `cannabis` enforces `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` (failing closed on `NULL` or deactivated membership `isActive = false`) and `@@unique([organizationId, id])` composite intra-tenant foreign keys, while server auth uses `@supabase/ssr` `await supabase.auth.getUser()` (banning `getSession()` and client-supplied organization/role trust).
* **NFR-RET-01 (Statutory 7-Year / 25-Year Record Retention Baseline [`ADR-0005`]):** All financial, tax, custody, scrap, production-run, destruction, and audit records enforce a minimum **`7-year`** retention baseline (satisfying the 6-year CRA *Income Tax Act*, *Excise Tax Act*, and *Excise Act, 2001* `s. 206` rules), and **`25 years`** for *Cannabis Regulations* `s. 248` serious adverse reaction annual summary reports.
* **NFR-CI-01 (Real PostgreSQL `erp_app_user` RLS Integration Tests & Deterministic Fail-Closed No-Stub Gate [`ADR-0005` & `ADR-0006`]):** CI/verification suites must execute real PostgreSQL RLS integration tests (`pnpm test:db:rls`) connected as `erp_app_user` (proving `0`-row fail-closed behavior without `withTenantTransaction`, revoked-membership denial, `shared.User` isolation, and strict cross-tenant isolation between `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`) and run the deterministic fail-closed verification script `pnpm verify:no-stubs` (`node scripts/verify-no-stubs.mjs`, replacing negated shell `grep` one-liners that fail open when paths are missing or `grep` exits `2`).
* **NFR-REL-01 (Tenant-Partitioned Offline Queue Durability):** The Yard & Cultivation PWA shall queue binary photo blobs and `CustodyEvent` mutations in IndexedDB (`Dexie.js`) partitioned by `organizationId`, display a persistent high-contrast badge showing the count of unsynced items, and block user logout, workspace switching, or cache clearing while unsynced mutations exist.
* **NFR-IDEM-01 (End-to-End Idempotency):** Every barcode scan, offline batch sync, eBay webhook, and WhatsApp webhook must supply a deterministic `Idempotency-Key` enforced by PostgreSQL unique constraints so network retries or double-taps never duplicate records.
* **NFR-ACC-01 (Gloved & Outdoor Usability):** All mobile scanner, nursery, and Vault POS screens must conform to WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios ($> 4.5:1$), use minimum $56 \times 56\text{ px}$ touch targets for gloved operation, and pair every color indicator (red/green) with distinct text, icon, and audio/haptic cues.
