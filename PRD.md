# PRD.md — Functional Specification & Phased Execution Backlog

## 1. Product Scope & Operational Mandate

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the functional workflows, role permissions, acceptance criteria, and phased delivery milestones for the **Unified Multi-Tenant Modular Monolith**, a reusable operations and compliance ERP platform serving **two independent legal corporations operating at completely separate physical facilities** ([`ADR-0004`](./docs/adr/0004-independent-legal-entities-and-physical-site-separation.md)). The corporations share **reusable software infrastructure only** (`shared` DB schema & `src/modules/shared-ops` — `8.5" × 11"` 10-MIL synthetic laser QR hang-tag sheet & optional ZPL generation, mobile camera barcode scanning, generic hierarchical `StorageLocation` custody, organization-scoped receipt-gated `Expense` & cash-custody abstractions, and tenant-segregated Cloudflare R2 storage), with **no shared physical warehouse, combined inventory, shared bank accounts or cash floats, shared operating permits, or assumed identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders**:

1. **Business A — Automotive & Marine Salvage (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — `salvage` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity):**
   * **Primary Physical Operating Location:** **Kemptville, Ontario (`KEMP` — `ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD`)** — vehicle & watercraft acquisition, dismantling, HTA Section 60 statutory register, 5-stream commodity scrap (`ScrapComponentLog`), 40ft container parts warehousing (`C01`–`C04`), 3-tap QR hang-tag custody, eBay Motors / B2B Mechanic Powertrain Sheets / Local Classifieds syndication, and Ontario Domestic CAD Checkout (`DOMESTIC_ORIGIN`).
   * **Ramsayville Retail, Shop & Fulfillment Concept (`RAMS` — `ZONE_RAMSAYVILLE_SHOP`, Salvage-Only, Subject to Verification):** Salvage-exclusive shop, quick-turn retail beater flip (`RETAIL_LOT_VEHICLE_SALE`), and East Ottawa customer pickup concept belonging **exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`** (architectural distinction preserved via configurable `StorageLocation` flags; operational status remains subject to confirmation).
   * **Pre-Paid Direct Export & Dormant Destination Bonded Vault (`POSV` — `DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT`):** 100% Pre-Paid Direct Export (`RULE-EXP-001`), with the Trinidad destination vault (`POSV`) retained as a dormant/future salvage-export capability until Phase 3 activation.
2. **Business B — Cannabis Cultivation & Processing (`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` — `cannabis` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity):**
   * **Exclusive Physical Operating Location:** **Brockville, Ontario (`BROCK` — `ZONE_BROCKVILLE_FACILITY`)** — licensed cannabis cultivation, genetics and propagation (mother plants, clone trays), cannabis processing ("moving the juice" / extraction & bulk lot transformation), lot/batch genealogy, inventory custody, QAP quality assurance, and Health Canada CTLS / CRA B300 regulatory reporting, subject to the corporation's actual Health Canada licence and authorized activities. *(Kemptville is strictly a salvage site and has zero cannabis nursery, greenhouse, cultivation, or processing operations.)*

Following the **Canada-First Logistics Pivot** (`ADR-0001`), **Synthetic Laser QR Tagging Standard** (`ADR-0002`), **Ontario Salvage Feasibility Audit & `WARN_ONLY` Physical Custody Agility Update** (`ADR-0003`), **Independent-Entity & Physical-Site Separation** (`ADR-0004`), and **RLS Pooler Safety, eBay Billing Agent & Post-2025 Cannabis Refinements** ([`ADR-0005`](./docs/adr/0005-rls-pooler-safety-ebay-billing-agent-and-post2025-cannabis.md)), delivery is sequenced to maximize immediate CAD cash velocity at Kemptville while preserving physical custody truth, Supavisor-safe corporate RLS isolation, and full Vertical-SaaS portability:

### 2.1 Organization Workspace Switcher & Multi-Tenant View Isolation

* **Default Single-Organization Isolation (Zero Assumed Cross-Entity Access):**
  * **Salvage-Scoped Memberships (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — e.g., `ARCHITECT_CFO`, `HUNTER_MECHANIC`, `ONTARIO_STRIPPER`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, `OVERSEAS_B2B_BUYER`):** Bound exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`. Users only see Salvage/Vault navigation, Kemptville primary locations (`KEMP-C01..C04`), proposed salvage-only `RAMS` (`PROPOSED_INACTIVE`) / dormant `POSV` (`DORMANT_FUTURE`) locations, and `salvage` schema views; they have zero visibility into `cannabis` tables or Brockville records, and the Workspace Switcher is hidden.
  * **Cannabis-Scoped Memberships (`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` — e.g., `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`, `NURSERY_CULTIVATION_TECH`, or a cannabis-entity `ARCHITECT_CFO`):** Bound exclusively to `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`. Users only see Brockville Cannabis Cultivation & Processing navigation, Brockville facility locations (`BROCK-*`), and `cannabis` schema views; they have zero visibility into `salvage` tables, Kemptville locations, or automotive cash sales, and the Workspace Switcher is hidden.
* **Explicit Multi-Organization Memberships (Never Inferred by Default):**
  * The platform **never** infers identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders across `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`legalEntityName = "Ontario Salvage OpCo Inc."`) and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (`legalEntityName = "Brockville Cannabis Operations Inc."`).
  * **If and only if** an authenticated user has been explicitly granted active `OrganizationMembership` rows in more than one `Organization` (in Phase 1A seed data, **disjoint single-org users are seeded by default** across `HUNTER_MECHANIC`, `ONTARIO_STRIPPER`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, `OVERSEAS_B2B_BUYER` in `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` only and `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`, `NURSERY_CULTIVATION_TECH` in `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` only, with **only `ARCHITECT_CFO`** granted active memberships in both organizations to test switcher visibility), the header renders the **Organization Workspace Switcher** (`[Auto & Marine Salvage]` $\leftrightarrow$ `[Cannabis Cultivation & Processing]`). Switching workspaces updates the validated active organization context passed into `withTenantTransaction` (`set_config('app.current_org_id', orgId, true)`), scoping all shared tables (`StorageLocation`, `Expense`, `CashCustodyTransfer`, `CustodyEvent`, `OutboxEvent`, `AuditLog`) and domain queries strictly to the selected corporation.

Phase 1B adds the Manifest V3 Chrome Extension (`FR-MKT-02`), Deal Analyzer (`FR-DEAL-01`), and **100% Pre-Paid Direct Export (`FR-LOG-01`, `FR-LOG-02` gated by `RULE-EXP-001`)**.

### Module A (Phase 1A): Asset & Part Intake, HTA s. 60 Register, EASR/Return QC & Append-Only Custody (`src/modules/dismantling`)

#### FR-INT-01: Multi-Category Donor & Whole-Unit Intake, HTA Section 60 Register, `O. Reg. 85/16` EASR Warning & Capital/Deal Gates
* **Description:** The system shall support registering three distinct `AssetCategory` types: `AUTOMOBILE` (17-character VIN), `WATERCRAFT` (12-character Hull Identification Number / HIN), and `SHOP_EQUIPMENT` (Manufacturer Serial Number) inside `withTenantTransaction`, maintaining a statutory Ontario *Highway Traffic Act* Section 60 Garage & Wrecking Register (`?view=hta-s60-register`) while enforcing capital/deal discipline gates with a 1-tap `cfoOverride`.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. When `assetCategory == 'AUTOMOBILE'`, the system calls `IAssetDecoderPort` (NHTSA vPIC) to auto-populate Year, Make, Model, Trim, and Engine, and defaults `steeringSide = 'LHD_ONLY'` for North American acquisitions.
  2. When `assetCategory` is `'WATERCRAFT'` or `'SHOP_EQUIPMENT'`, the system sets `roadBrand = 'NOT_APPLICABLE'`, `steeringSide = 'UNIVERSAL'`, and bypasses all Ontario HTA Section 60, `O. Reg. 85/16` ELV EASR, and DriveON road-inspection checks.
  3. **Ontario HTA Section 60 Statutory Register, `O. Reg. 85/16` EASR Threshold & `WARN_ONLY` Agility (`complianceEnforcementMode`):** Captures `acquisitionSource`, `sellerFullName`, `sellerAddress`, `sellerPhotoIdStorageKey` (`R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`), `ontarioPermitNumber` (green-slip permit #), and `declaredStatutoryPurpose`, plus optional DriveON 10-day re-inspection fail-sheet timestamps (`driveOnInitialFailAt`, `driveOnReinspectionDeadlineAt = +10 days`, `driveOnFailSheetStorageKey`). In `"WARN_ONLY"` mode (Phase 1A default):
     * Missing HTA s. 60 fields on an `AUTOMOBILE` **never block intake (`422`)**—the vehicle is created and `'MISSING_HTA_S60_DOCS'` is appended to `Vehicle.complianceWarnings` (`422` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
     * Intaking active `AUTOMOBILE` ELV `#3+` while `complianceThresholds.easrRegistrationNumber == null` (`O. Reg. 85/16` $> 2$ ELV threshold) appends `'MISSING_EASR_REGISTRATION_WARNING'` without blocking intake (`422 EASR_REGISTRATION_REQUIRED` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
     * Routing intake to a `StorageLocation` with `locationOperationalStatus == 'PROPOSED_INACTIVE'` (such as `RAMS`) appends `'PROPOSED_INACTIVE_LOCATION_WARNING'` without blocking intake (`422 LOCATION_PROPOSED_INACTIVE` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
  4. **Enforced Capital, Deal-Cap, Lane-Pause & Capacity Gates with 1-Tap `cfoOverride`:** Enforces Tranche 1 `workingCapitalGate` (`$22,000 CAD` active principal minus `netHstTrustLiability` and `$7,500 CAD` `minCashReserveFloorCadMinor` = `$14,500 CAD` launch buying power), `maxDonorDismantleLandedCadMinor` (`$3,200 CAD` cap on `DISMANTLE_AND_EXPORT`), `enableHighEndRebuildLane = false` (Year-1 pause on `HIGH_END_REBUILD`), and destination `StorageLocation.maxActiveAssetCapacity`. Every capital/deal/capacity gate supports a **1-Tap `cfoOverride`** (`cfoOverride: { approved: true, reasonNote }` by `ARCHITECT_CFO`, stamping `cfoGateApprovedById`, `cfoGateApprovedAt`, `cfoGateOverrideReason`, and `AuditLog`).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Given Ontario LHD donor SUV VINs (`DISMANTLE_AND_EXPORT`), retail beater VINs (`QUICK_TURN_BEATER`), or whole watercraft/equipment identifiers, intake enforces capital/deal/capacity gates (or records 1-tap `cfoOverride`), evaluates `RULE-ENV-001` (`COMPLIANCE_WARNING_UNDRAINED_FARM`, `MISSING_EASR_REGISTRATION_WARNING`, `PROPOSED_INACTIVE_LOCATION_WARNING` in `"WARN_ONLY"`), creates the `Vehicle` + initial `CustodyEvent`, and serves a read-only HTA s. 60 Statutory Register view (`GET /api/salvage/vehicles?view=hta-s60-register`).

#### FR-INT-02: 3-Tap Part Strip, QR Hang-Tag Binding, TDG Hazmat Tagging, Return QC Hold & 5-Stream Commodity Scrap
* **Description:** Workers stripping a donor vehicle within `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`KEMP`, or `RAMS` if verified active) shall loop a pre-printed weatherproof `10-MIL` synthetic laser QR hang tag (`PRT-XXXXXX`, fastened with a `-40°C` black UV zip tie per `RULE-TAG-001`) onto each physical component, capture 3 photos, classify steering-side compatibility and TDG hazmat code, scan a destination storage bin QR card (`KEMP-C01-R01-S01-B01`), inspect any returned parts in `RESTOCKED_QC_HOLD`, and log separated scrap commodity streams (`ScrapComponentLog`) before hulk clearance.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. All uploaded product photos are stripped of EXIF GPS coordinates and camera metadata before being written to `R2_PUBLIC_MEDIA_BUCKET` (`RULE-MKT-002` Kemptville geolocation stealth).
  2. Each stripped part is tracked across five orthogonal state axes (`custodyState` [including `RESTOCKED_QC_HOLD`], `availabilityState`, `ebaySyncState` [including `CSV_EXPORTED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION`], `classifiedsSyncState`, `complianceState`) and tagged with `SteeringSideCompatibility` (`UNIVERSAL` vs `LHD_ONLY`) and `TdgHazmatCode` (`NONE | UN3268_AIRBAG | UN0503_PYROTECHNIC | UN2794_WET_BATTERY | UN3480_LITHIUM_BATTERY`).
  3. **TDG Hazmat & Safety Screen:** Any airbag module or pyrotechnic seatbelt pretensioner (`UN3268_AIRBAG`, `UN0503_PYROTECHNIC`) must be flagged `isHazmatOrAirbag = true` and automatically placed into `complianceState = 'QUARANTINED_RECALL_OR_DG'`. Any lead-acid or lithium battery (`UN2794_WET_BATTERY`, `UN3480_LITHIUM_BATTERY`) may be sold locally if non-quarantined, but all items with `tdgHazmatCode !== 'NONE'` are hard-blocked from eBay parcel shipping export.
  4. **Online vs. Offline Initial Custody State & Anti-Part-Swap Return QC Hold (`RESTOCKED_QC_HOLD`):** Completing the 3-Tap Part Strip online with both `PRT-XXXXXX` and a valid `toLocationBarcode` creates the `Part` and atomically transitions `custodyState` to `SHELVED_ORIGIN_CONTAINER` (recording `CustodyEvent`). * **Description:** Upon entering the final customs broker assessment (`assessedDutyTtdMinor`, `assessedVatTtdMinor`, port drayage, and ocean freight) on the `Shipment`, the system allocates landed costs across all parts and whole units on the shipment (weighted by declared FOB value and weight), computes each item's minimum Cash-on-Delivery price (`vaultFloorPriceTtdMinor`), and tracks the shipment across the **3-Tier Shipment Profitability Model** (Tier 1 Projected Contribution CAD, Tier 2 Realized Operating Profit TTD/CAD, and Tier 3 Repatriated Cash Return CAD as defined in `DOMAIN-RULES.md` Section 4.4):

* **Business Rules:**

  4. The admin must upload a payment proof photo (cash count or verified bank wire receipt) to `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`.
  5. **Trinidad FIU / CDD Compliance Gate (`RULE-COMP-FIU-01` — Encrypted Private ID Storage & 30-Day Rolling Window):** If a single transaction or linked buyer transactions total $\ge \text{TT\$50,000}$ (`5000000` minor units), the UI enforces uploading a photo/scan of the buyer's government ID (`buyerIdDocumentStorageKey` in `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`) and capturing the AES-256-GCM application-encrypted ID type and number (`buyerIdNumberEncrypted`). ## 6. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) & Quality Gates

* **NFR-SEC-01 (Tenant-Prefixed Storage Isolation):** `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET` shall block all public access, and all object keys across both `R2_PUBLIC_MEDIA_BUCKET` and `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET` shall be namespaced by `{organizationId}/...`. Receipts, UVIPs, bank deposit slips, encrypted customer government ID documents (`buyerIdDocumentStorageKey`), Lab CoA PDFs (`labCoaStorageKey`), and cannabis destruction evidence photos (`evidencePhotoStorageKey`) may only be accessed via server-generated pre-signed URLs (maximum TTL 15 minutes) after verifying active `OrganizationMembership` for that `organizationId`, logged in `AuditLog`.
* **NFR-SEC-02 (Supavisor-Safe `erp_app_user` `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` & Composite Tenant FK Firewall [`ADR-0005`]):** Because Prisma connects directly over TCP rather than PostgREST, `auth.jwt()` RLS policies are banned. Application queries run under dedicated runtime role `erp_app_user` (`NOBYPASSRLS`, `plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan`) over Supavisor transaction pooling (`?pgbouncer=true` on port `6543`) inside `withTenantTransaction(prisma, ctx, fn)`, which executes transaction-local `set_config('app.current_org_id', ..., true)` (`is_local = true`). Every tenant-scoped table across `shared`, `salvage`, and `cannabis` enforces `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` evaluating `"organizationId" = shared.current_org_id()` (failing closed on `NULL`) and `@@unique([organizationId, id])` composite intra-tenant foreign keys, while server auth uses `@supabase/ssr` `await supabase.auth.getUser()` (banning `getSession()`).
* **NFR-RET-01 (Statutory 7-Year / 25-Year Record Retention Baseline [`ADR-0005`]):** All financial, tax, custody, scrap, production-run, destruction, and audit records enforce a minimum **`7-year`** retention baseline (satisfying the 6-year CRA *Income Tax Act*, *Excise Tax Act*, and *Excise Act, 2001* `s. 206` rules), and **`25 years`** for *Cannabis Regulations* `s. 248` serious adverse reaction annual summary reports.
* **NFR-CI-01 (Real PostgreSQL `erp_app_user` RLS Integration Tests & Zero-Stub CI Gate [`ADR-0005`]):** CI/verification suites must execute real PostgreSQL RLS integration tests connected as `erp_app_user` (proving `0`-row fail-closed behavior without `withTenantTransaction` and strict cross-tenant isolation between `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`) and run `! grep -rnE "not wired|TODO.*compose" src/ app/` to fail any build containing unwired `compose.ts` stubs.
* **NFR-REL-01 (Tenant-Partitioned Offline Queue Durability):** The Yard & Cultivation PWA shall queue binary photo blobs and `CustodyEvent` mutations in IndexedDB (`Dexie.js`) partitioned by `organizationId`, display a persistent high-contrast badge showing the count of unsynced items, and block user logout, workspace switching, or cache clearing while unsynced mutations exist.
* **NFR-IDEM-01 (End-to-End Idempotency):** Every barcode scan, offline batch sync, eBay webhook, and WhatsApp webhook must supply a deterministic `Idempotency-Key` enforced by PostgreSQL unique constraints so network retries or double-taps never duplicate records.
* **NFR-ACC-01 (Gloved & Outdoor Usability):** All mobile scanner, nursery, and Vault POS screens must conform to WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios ($> 4.5:1$), use minimum $56 \times 56\text{ px}$ touch targets for gloved operation, and pair every color indicator (red/green) with distinct text, icon, and audio/haptic cues.
