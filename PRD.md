# PRD.md — Functional Specification & Phased Execution Backlog

## 1. Product Scope & Operational Mandate

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the functional workflows, role permissions, acceptance criteria, and phased delivery milestones for the **Unified Multi-Tenant Modular Monolith**, a reusable operations and compliance ERP platform serving **two independent legal corporations operating at completely separate physical facilities** ([`ADR-0004`](./docs/adr/0004-independent-legal-entities-and-physical-site-separation.md)). The corporations share **reusable software infrastructure only** (`shared` DB schema & `src/modules/shared-ops` — `8.5" × 11"` 10-MIL synthetic laser QR hang-tag sheet & optional ZPL generation, mobile camera barcode scanning, generic hierarchical `StorageLocation` custody, organization-scoped receipt-gated `Expense` & cash-custody abstractions, and tenant-segregated Cloudflare R2 storage), with **no shared physical warehouse, combined inventory, shared bank accounts or cash floats, shared operating permits, or assumed identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders**:

1. **Business A — Automotive & Marine Salvage (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — `salvage` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity):**
   * **Primary Physical Operating Location:** **Kemptville, Ontario (`KEMP` — `ZONE_CONTAINER_PAD`)** — vehicle & watercraft acquisition, dismantling, HTA Section 60 statutory register, 5-stream commodity scrap (`ScrapComponentLog`), 40ft container parts warehousing (`C01`–`C04`), 3-tap QR hang-tag custody, eBay Motors / B2B Mechanic Powertrain Sheets / Local Classifieds syndication, and Ontario Domestic CAD Checkout (`DOMESTIC_ORIGIN`).
   * **Ramsayville Retail, Shop & Fulfillment Concept (`RAMS` — `ZONE_RAMSAYVILLE_SHOP`, Salvage-Only, Subject to Verification):** Salvage-exclusive shop, quick-turn retail beater flip (`RETAIL_LOT_VEHICLE_SALE`), and East Ottawa customer pickup concept belonging **exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`** (architectural distinction preserved via configurable `StorageLocation` flags; operational status remains subject to confirmation).
   * **Pre-Paid Direct Export & Dormant Destination Bonded Vault (`POSV` — `DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT`):** 100% Pre-Paid Direct Export (`RULE-EXP-001`), with the Trinidad destination vault (`POSV`) retained as a dormant/future salvage-export capability until Phase 3 activation.
2. **Business B — Cannabis Cultivation & Processing (`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` — `cannabis` DB schema, Independent Legal Entity):**
   * **Exclusive Physical Operating Location:** **Brockville, Ontario (`BROCK` — `ZONE_BROCKVILLE_FACILITY`)** — licensed cannabis cultivation, genetics and propagation (mother plants, clone trays), cannabis processing ("moving the juice" / extraction & bulk lot transformation), lot/batch genealogy, inventory custody, QAP quality assurance, and Health Canada CTLS / CRA B300 regulatory reporting, subject to the corporation's actual Health Canada licence and authorized activities. *(Kemptville is strictly a salvage site and has zero cannabis nursery, greenhouse, cultivation, or processing operations.)*

Following the **Canada-First Logistics Pivot** (`ADR-0001`), **Synthetic Laser QR Tagging Standard** (`ADR-0002`), **Ontario Salvage Feasibility Audit & `WARN_ONLY` Physical Custody Agility Update** (`ADR-0003`), and **Independent-Entity & Physical-Site Separation** (`ADR-0004`), delivery is sequenced to maximize immediate CAD cash velocity at Kemptville while preserving physical custody truth, independent corporate isolation, and full Vertical-SaaS portability:
* **Phase 1A (Days 1–7 — Kemptville Salvage Launch MVP):** Shared Software Core (`shared` schema) + Kemptville Salvage Intake, Dismantling, HTA s. 60 Register & 5-Stream Scrap (`FR-INT-01`, `FR-INT-02` with `complianceEnforcementMode = "WARN_ONLY"` physical custody agility and 1-tap `cfoOverride` on capital/deal gates), `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` 3-Tranche `$44k` Senior Loan (`$22k / $12k / $10k`), `ITA s. 78(1)` Deferred Accrual & Tiered Receipt/Voucher Operator Float Subledger (`FR-EXP-01`, `FR-EXP-02`), **Zero-Typing Domestic Syndication Bridge (`FR-MKT-00` — eBay Seller Hub Bulk CSV, Day-1 B2B Mechanic Powertrain Sheet & Bylaw-Stealth Local Classifieds Queue)**, and **Ontario Domestic Mobile Checkout (`FR-CHK-01` in `DOMESTIC_ORIGIN` CAD mode with 13% HST Trust Ring-Fencing & atomic Anti-Double-Sale Delist Sync)**.
* **Phase 1B (Days 8–21 — Local Chrome Extension, Deal Analyzer & Pre-Paid Direct Export):** Manifest V3 Local FB Marketplace / Kijiji Chrome Extension (`FR-MKT-02`), "Bad Cop" Pre-Auction Deal Analyzer (`FR-DEAL-01`, `FR-DEAL-02`), and **100% Pre-Paid Direct Export Consolidation & Broker Customs Package (`FR-LOG-01`, `FR-LOG-02` gated by `RULE-EXP-001`)** for upfront-paid overseas orders (e.g., watercraft, shop equipment, or pre-sold pallets).
* **Phase 2A (Days 22–45 — Automated eBay Inventory API Sync, Offline Stripper PWA & GL Sync):** Pulled-forward **Automated eBay Inventory API + Browse API Comp Floor + Inbound Order Webhooks** (automating live `quantity = 0` delisting and pick-pull alerts), Offline-first IndexedDB (`Dexie.js`) Yard Stripper PWA, and QuickBooks Online / Xero outbound GL sync (`IAccountingGlPort`).
* **Phase 2B (Days 45–90 — Brockville Cannabis Cultivation, Processing & CTLS Compliance):** Cannabis Compliance Module (`cannabis` schema, `FR-CAN-01` to `FR-CAN-04`) for **`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` at Brockville (`BROCK`)**, reusing the `shared-ops` **software** components (camera QR scanner, `10-MIL` synthetic laser QR tag sheet & optional ZPL generator, hierarchical `StorageLocation` engine, and organization-scoped receipt-gated `Expense` ledger) as Brockville facility operations come online under its Health Canada licence.
* **Phase 3 (Months 3–6 / Overseas Vault Expansion — Assistive AI Vision & Dormant Destination Bonded Vault Activation):** BullMQ Assistive AI Vision OCR/grading (`FR-AI-01`), plus dormant `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` Destination Bonded Vault workflows (`FR-VLT-01` to `FR-VLT-05`: landed vault check-in, TTD landed-cost floor allocation, MFA `aal2` COD release, weekly blind cycle counts, FX repatriation ledger, Two-Step HMAC Gate Token) and the Two-Tab Live Container B2B Storefront (`FR-MKT-01`).

### 2.1 Organization Workspace Switcher & Multi-Tenant View Isolation

* **Default Single-Organization Isolation (Zero Assumed Cross-Entity Access):**
  * **Salvage-Scoped Memberships (`ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` — e.g., `ARCHITECT_CFO`, `HUNTER_MECHANIC`, `ONTARIO_STRIPPER`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, `OVERSEAS_B2B_BUYER`):** Bound exclusively to `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`. Users only see Salvage/Vault navigation, Kemptville primary locations (`KEMP-C01..C04`), optional salvage-only `RAMS` / dormant `POSV` locations, and `salvage` schema views; they have zero visibility into `cannabis` tables or Brockville records, and the Workspace Switcher is hidden.
  * **Cannabis-Scoped Memberships (`ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` — e.g., `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`, `NURSERY_CULTIVATION_TECH`, or a cannabis-entity `ARCHITECT_CFO`):** Bound exclusively to `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`. Users only see Brockville Cannabis Cultivation & Processing navigation, Brockville facility locations (`BROCK-*`), and `cannabis` schema views; they have zero visibility into `salvage` tables, Kemptville locations, or automotive cash sales, and the Workspace Switcher is hidden.
* **Explicit Multi-Organization Memberships (Never Inferred by Default):**
  * The platform **never** infers identical shareholders, directors, employees, or licence holders across `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`legalEntityName = "Ontario Salvage OpCo Inc."`) and `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (`legalEntityName = "Brockville Cannabis Operations Inc."`).
  * **If and only if** an authenticated user has been explicitly granted active `OrganizationMembership` rows in more than one `Organization` (in Phase 1A seed data, **disjoint single-org users are seeded by default** across `HUNTER_MECHANIC`, `ONTARIO_STRIPPER`, `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN`, `OVERSEAS_B2B_BUYER` in `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` only and `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`, `NURSERY_CULTIVATION_TECH` in `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` only, with **only `ARCHITECT_CFO`** granted active memberships in both organizations to test switcher visibility), the header renders the **Organization Workspace Switcher** (`[Auto & Marine Salvage]` $\leftrightarrow$ `[Cannabis Cultivation & Processing]`). Switching workspaces refreshes the active JWT `organization_id` claim so all shared tables (`StorageLocation`, `Expense`, `CashCustodyTransfer`, `CustodyEvent`, `OutboxEvent`, `AuditLog`) and domain queries are strictly scoped to the selected corporation.

## 3. Phase 1 Functional Requirements (Canada-First Kemptville Salvage, eBay Syndication & Pre-Paid Export MVP)

Phase 1B adds the Manifest V3 Chrome Extension (`FR-MKT-02`), Deal Analyzer (`FR-DEAL-01`), and **100% Pre-Paid Direct Export (`FR-LOG-01`, `FR-LOG-02` gated by `RULE-EXP-001`)**.

### Module A (Phase 1A): Asset & Part Intake, HTA s. 60 Register, 5-Stream Scrap & Append-Only Custody (`src/modules/dismantling`)

#### FR-INT-01: Multi-Category Donor & Whole-Unit Intake, HTA Section 60 Register & Capital/Deal Gates
* **Description:** The system shall support registering three distinct `AssetCategory` types: `AUTOMOBILE` (17-character VIN), `WATERCRAFT` (12-character Hull Identification Number / HIN), and `SHOP_EQUIPMENT` (Manufacturer Serial Number), maintaining a statutory Ontario *Highway Traffic Act* Section 60 Garage & Wrecking Register (`?view=hta-s60-register`) while enforcing capital/deal discipline gates with a 1-tap `cfoOverride`.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. When `assetCategory == 'AUTOMOBILE'`, the system calls `IAssetDecoderPort` (NHTSA vPIC) to auto-populate Year, Make, Model, Trim, and Engine, and defaults `steeringSide = 'LHD_ONLY'` for North American acquisitions.
  2. When `assetCategory` is `'WATERCRAFT'` or `'SHOP_EQUIPMENT'`, the system sets `roadBrand = 'NOT_APPLICABLE'`, `steeringSide = 'UNIVERSAL'`, and bypasses all Ontario HTA Section 60 and DriveON road-inspection checks.
  3. **Ontario HTA Section 60 Statutory Register & `WARN_ONLY` Agility (`complianceEnforcementMode`):** Captures `acquisitionSource` (`LICENSED_AUCTION | PRIVATE_DRIVEWAY_BUY | DEALER_WHOLESALE | TRADE_IN`), `sellerFullName`, `sellerAddress`, `sellerPhotoIdStorageKey` (`R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`), `ontarioPermitNumber` (green-slip permit #), and `declaredStatutoryPurpose` (`WRECKING_PARTS_ONLY | RETAIL_RESALE | EXPORT_WHOLE`), plus optional DriveON 10-day re-inspection fail-sheet timestamps (`driveOnInitialFailAt`, `driveOnReinspectionDeadlineAt = +10 days`, `driveOnFailSheetStorageKey`). In `"WARN_ONLY"` mode (Phase 1A default), missing HTA s. 60 fields on an `AUTOMOBILE` **never block intake (`422`)**—the vehicle is created and `'MISSING_HTA_S60_DOCS'` is appended to `Vehicle.complianceWarnings` (`422` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
  4. **Enforced Capital, Deal-Cap, Lane-Pause & Capacity Gates with 1-Tap `cfoOverride`:** Enforces Tranche 1 `workingCapitalGate` (`$22,000 CAD` active principal minus `$7,500 CAD` `minCashReserveFloorCadMinor` = `$14,500 CAD` launch buying power), `maxDonorDismantleLandedCadMinor` (`$3,200 CAD` cap on `DISMANTLE_AND_EXPORT`), `enableHighEndRebuildLane = false` (Year-1 pause on `HIGH_END_REBUILD`), and destination `StorageLocation.maxActiveAssetCapacity` (data-driven per zone). Every capital/deal/capacity gate supports a **1-Tap `cfoOverride`** (`cfoOverride: { approved: true, reasonNote }` by `ARCHITECT_CFO`, stamping `cfoGateApprovedById`, `cfoGateApprovedAt`, `cfoGateOverrideReason`, and `AuditLog`).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Given Ontario LHD donor SUV VINs (`DISMANTLE_AND_EXPORT`), retail beater VINs (`QUICK_TURN_BEATER`), or whole watercraft/equipment identifiers, intake enforces capital/deal/capacity gates (or records 1-tap `cfoOverride`), evaluates `RULE-ENV-001` (`COMPLIANCE_WARNING_UNDRAINED_FARM` if routed to a location where `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling == false` in `"WARN_ONLY"`), creates the `Vehicle` + initial `CustodyEvent`, and serves a read-only HTA s. 60 Statutory Register view (`GET /api/salvage/vehicles?view=hta-s60-register`).

#### FR-INT-02: 3-Tap Part Strip, QR Hang-Tag Binding, Configurable Environmental Dismantling Flag & 5-Stream Commodity Scrap
* **Description:** Workers stripping a donor vehicle within `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT` (`KEMP`, or `RAMS` if verified active) shall loop a pre-printed weatherproof `10-MIL` synthetic laser QR hang tag (`PRT-XXXXXX`, fastened with a `-40°C` black UV zip tie per `RULE-TAG-001`) onto each physical component, capture 3 photos, classify steering-side compatibility, scan a destination storage bin QR card (`KEMP-C01-R01-S01-B01`), and log separated scrap commodity streams (`ScrapComponentLog`) before hulk clearance.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. All uploaded product photos are stripped of EXIF GPS coordinates and camera metadata before being written to `R2_PUBLIC_MEDIA_BUCKET` (`RULE-MKT-002` Kemptville geolocation stealth).
  2. Each stripped part is tracked across five orthogonal state axes (`custodyState`, `availabilityState`, `ebaySyncState`, `classifiedsSyncState`, `complianceState`) and tagged with `SteeringSideCompatibility` (`UNIVERSAL` vs `LHD_ONLY`).
  3. **Hazmat & Safety Screen:** Any airbag module (`8708.95`) or uncertified hazardous component must be flagged `isHazmatOrAirbag = true` and automatically placed into `complianceState = 'QUARANTINED_RECALL_OR_DG'`.
  4. **Online vs. Offline Initial Custody State Transition:** Completing the 3-Tap Part Strip online with both `PRT-XXXXXX` and a valid `toLocationBarcode` creates the `Part` and atomically transitions `custodyState` to `SHELVED_ORIGIN_CONTAINER` (recording `CustodyEvent`).
  5. **Data-Driven Multi-Tenant Location Isolation (`RULE-ZONE-001`) & Environmental Flag (`RULE-ENV-001` / `O. Reg. 85/16`):** Cross-tenant scans where `StorageLocation.organizationId` or `StorageLocation.allowedOrgType` does not match the entity (`SALVAGE_AND_EXPORT` vs `CANNABIS_NURSERY_AND_PROCESSING`, e.g., attempting to scan a salvage item into a Brockville `BROCK-*` cannabis location) are **ALWAYS hard-blocked (`422 SITE_ZONING_VIOLATION`)**. Scanning a whole `AUTOMOBILE` or an undrained fluid-bearing powertrain `Part` (`Vehicle.fluidsDrainedAt == null`) into a location where `allowsWholeVehicleDismantling == false` **succeeds** in `"WARN_ONLY"` mode and appends `'COMPLIANCE_WARNING_UNDRAINED_FARM'` to `CustodyEvent.complianceWarnings` and entity `complianceWarnings` (or throws `422 ENVIRONMENTAL_ZONING_VIOLATION` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"` mode).
  6. **5-Stream Commodity Scrap Protocol (`ScrapComponentLog`) & Catalytic Converter Check:** Operators log separated scrap sales across `CATALYTIC_CONVERTER`, `ALUMINUM_ALLOY_WHEELS`, `COPPER_WIRING_HARNESS`, `ROTORS_AND_BATTERY`, and `BARE_STEEL_SHELL` (`weightKg`, `payoutCadMinor`, `recyclerName`, `recyclerTicketStorageKey` in `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`, and `cashCustodyState`: `SETTLED_ORIGIN_CORP_BANK` or `HELD_IN_OPERATOR_FLOAT`), automatically rolling up `Vehicle.scrapRecoveryCadMinor`, running Stage 2 donor cost true-up across unsold parts, and crediting `AvailableWorkingCapital_CAD` (and `RunningOperatorFloat_CAD` when paid in cash). Transitioning an `AUTOMOBILE` to `SCRAPPED_METAL_CLEARED` without a reconciled catalytic converter (`MISSING_AT_INTAKE`, `LOGGED_SCRAP_SALE`, or `SHELVED_AS_PART`) succeeds in `"WARN_ONLY"` mode and appends `'UNRECONCILED_CATALYTIC_CONVERTER'` (`422` in `"STRICT_BLOCK"`).
  7. **Batch `8.5" × 11"` Synthetic Laser QR Tag Sheet Generation (`RULE-TAG-001`):** `shared-ops` generates printable Letter-size PDF and SVG sheets in **`3×4` (12-up)** and **`4×4` (16-up)** grids for matte `10-MIL` waterproof synthetic laser cardstock (`PRT-XXXXXX` hang tags and 2-hole steel rack upright bin cards for `KEMP-C01-*`), keeping Zebra ZPL II output as an optional secondary adapter format.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Scanning a duplicate `PRT-XXXXXX` code already bound to another part returns HTTP `409 Conflict` and triggers an immediate visual/audio alert.
  * Every location scan (for either a `Part` or a whole `Vehicle`) appends an immutable `CustodyEvent` containing the client's UUIDv7 `idempotencyKey` and `expectedVersion`.

#### FR-MKT-00: Zero-Typing Domestic Syndication Bridge (Phase 1A Day-1 MVP)

* **Business Rules & Outputs:**

  2. **Lab CoA Gate (`labCoaStorageKey`):** Bulk extract or dried flower lots cannot be cleared for B2B wholesale release while `complianceHold == true` or without a private Lab CoA PDF (`labCoaStorageKey` in `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`) verified by `CANNABIS_QAP_COMPLIANCE`.
  3. **Strict Audit Isolation:** CTLS and CRA B300 export queries run exclusively against `cannabis.*` and `shared.*` rows where `organizationId == ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC`, guaranteeing zero leakage of `salvage.*` records.

#### FR-VLT-01: Landed Container Check-In & Discrepancy Alerting

  * Any manifested SKU not scanned into the vault within 24 hours of unstuffing flags a **Transit Shrinkage Exception** on the CFO dashboard.

#### FR-VLT-02: Landed Cost Allocation, TTD Floor Pricing & 3-Tier Shipment P&L
* **Description:** Upon entering the final customs broker assessment (`assessedDutyTtdMinor`, `assessedVatTtdMinor`, port drayage, and ocean freight) on the `Shipment`, the system allocates landed costs across all parts and whole units on the shipment (weighted by declared FOB value and weight), computes each item's minimum Cash-on-Delivery price (`vaultFloorPriceTtdMinor`), and tracks the shipment across the **3-Tier Shipment Profitability Model** (Tier 1 Projected Contribution CAD, Tier 2 Realized Operating Profit TTD/CAD, and Tier 3 Repatriated Cash Return CAD as defined in `DOMAIN-RULES.md` Section 4.4):
  $$\text{Floor}_{\text{TTD}} = \frac{\left(\text{CostBasis}_{\text{CAD}} \times (1 + \text{TargetMarkup}) \times \text{FX}_{\text{CAD\_to\_USD}} \times \text{FX}_{\text{shadow}}\right) + \text{AllocatedLandedTaxesAndFreight}_{\text{TTD}}}{1 - r_{\text{comm}}}$$
* **Business Rules:**
  * All tariff rates, VAT percentages ($12.5\%$), broker commission rates ($5\%\text{–}10\%$, calculated strictly on pre-tax `subtotalMinor` per `RULE-PRC-002`), and shadow FX reserve rates are read from the versioned configuration register (`DOMAIN-RULES.md`), never hardcoded in business logic.

#### FR-VLT-03: Strict COD Verification, 3-Tier Negotiation Corridor & Phased Gate Release
* **Description:** When a buyer or commissioned broker arrives at the Destination Vault to purchase parts or watercraft, the system enforces a hard separation between sales selection and payment verification while providing a bounded Vault Admin negotiation corridor and counter commission netting.
* **Business Rules:**
  1. Only `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN` or `ARCHITECT_CFO` with an active MFA-verified JWT session (`aal2`) can execute `verifyCodPayment` (`checkoutStrictnessMode == 'BONDED_VAULT_STRICT'` default for `ORG_SALVAGE_EXPORT`, configurable to `'AUDIT_ENFORCED'` or `'STANDARD_POS'` for external SaaS yards).
  2. **3-Tier Price Floor Evaluation & Negotiation Corridor (`floorPriceAtCheckoutMinor`):**
     * **Tier 1 (At or Above Floor):** `grossCollectedMinor >= floorPriceAtCheckoutMinor` $\rightarrow$ Passes automatically (`discountReasonCode = null`).
     * **Tier 2 (Within Admin Negotiation Corridor):** Discount (`discountAmountMinor`) is $\le \min(\text{vaultAdminMaxDiscountCapTtdMinor } [\text{TT\$750}],\; \max(\text{vaultAdminSmallRoundingAllowanceTtdMinor } [\text{TT\$100}],\; \text{floorPriceAtCheckoutMinor} \times 0.08))$ $\rightarrow$ Allowed for `TRINIDAD_VAULT_ADMIN` (or `HUNTER_MECHANIC` in Ontario under `domesticAdminDiscountToleranceBps = 1000` [10%]) **if and only if** a valid `discountReasonCode` (`"CASH_ROUNDING" | "BUNDLE_DISCOUNT" | "TRANSIT_OR_SHOP_DAMAGE" | "AGED_INVENTORY_CLEARANCE"`) is selected.
     * **Tier 3 (Beyond Admin Corridor — Hard Lock / CFO Override Required):** Discount exceeds the Admin Corridor $\rightarrow$ Rejects with `422 FLOOR_PRICE_OVERRIDE_REQUIRED` unless `actorRole === 'ARCHITECT_CFO'` or a valid `cfoOverrideApprovedById` + `cfoOverrideReasonNote` (via CFO Override PIN/approval) is supplied.
  3. **Broker Commission Deducted at Counter Toggle (`isBrokerCommissionDeductedAtCounter`):** When `isBrokerCommissionDeductedAtCounter == true`, the broker retained their commission directly from the cash handed over at the counter, so the physical cash added to `HELD_IN_DESTINATION_VAULT` is `grossCollectedMinor - brokerCommissionMinor` (keeping physical vault safe cash 100% accurate).
  4. The admin must upload a payment proof photo (cash count or verified bank wire receipt) to `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`.
  5. **Trinidad FIU / CDD Compliance Gate (`RULE-COMP-FIU-01` — Encrypted Private ID Storage & 30-Day Rolling Window):** If a single transaction or linked buyer transactions total $\ge \text{TT\$50,000}$ (`5000000` minor units), the UI enforces uploading a photo/scan of the buyer's government ID (`buyerIdDocumentStorageKey` in `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET`) and capturing the AES-256-GCM application-encrypted ID type and number (`buyerIdNumberEncrypted`). Linked transactions are evaluated deterministically across all non-voided `SaleTransaction` records (`marketNode == 'DESTINATION_EXPORT_VAULT'` and `isVoidedOrReversed == false`) within a **rolling 30-calendar-day window (`createdAt >= now() - 30 days`)** matching the same `buyerPhoneE164`, normalized `lower(trim(buyerName))`, or `buyerIdNumberEncrypted` (triggering the Government ID gate at $\ge \text{TT\$50,000}$ and the mandatory Large Cash Transaction Alert at $\ge \text{TT\$90,000}$).
  6. **Atomic Release vs. Two-Step Gate Token:** While counter and exit are operated by the same team, payment verification atomically stamps `gateReleasedAt = now()`, transitioning child parts to `custodyState = 'SOLD_AND_RELEASED'` (and whole assets to `lifecycleState = 'SOLD_EXPORT_COD'`). When dedicated exit guards operate separately from counter staff, verification issues a **Single-Use HMAC-SHA256 Gate Release QR Token** (`gateTokenNonce`, valid for 4 hours) consumed at the warehouse exit (returning HTTP `410 Gone` on replay).

#### FR-VLT-04: Commercial Bank Cap, Cash Custody Transfers & FX Repatriation Batch Ledger
* **Description:** Tracks TTD cash deposited from the Vault safe (`HELD_IN_DESTINATION_VAULT`) into Trinidadian commercial banks (`DEPOSITED_DESTINATION_BANK`) via `CashCustodyTransfer` and logs conversion batches (`CurrencySettlement`) back into CAD/USD (`SETTLED_ORIGIN_CORP_BANK`).

#### FR-VLT-05: Weekly Blind Cycle Counts & Bank Deposit Reconciliation
* **Description:** Once per week (or triggered on demand by `ARCHITECT_CFO`), generates a `CycleCountTask` batch selecting up to 10 random `SHELVED_DESTINATION_VAULT` parts/vehicles without revealing the expected shelf bin prior to arrival.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) & Quality Gates

* **NFR-SEC-01 (Tenant-Prefixed Storage Isolation):** `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET` shall block all public access, and all object keys across both `R2_PUBLIC_MEDIA_BUCKET` and `R2_PRIVATE_EVIDENCE_BUCKET` shall be namespaced by `{organizationId}/...`. Receipts, UVIPs, bank deposit slips, encrypted customer government ID documents (`buyerIdDocumentStorageKey`), Lab CoA PDFs (`labCoaStorageKey`), and cannabis destruction evidence photos (`evidencePhotoStorageKey`) may only be accessed via server-generated pre-signed URLs (maximum TTL 15 minutes) after verifying active `OrganizationMembership` for that `organizationId`, logged in `AuditLog`.
* **NFR-SEC-02 (Multi-Schema & Default-Deny RLS Audit Firewall):** PostgreSQL tables are isolated across `shared`, `salvage`, and `cannabis` schemas with default-deny Supabase RLS policies enforcing `organizationId = (auth.jwt() ->> 'organization_id')::uuid` across all three schemas so cross-tenant access is prohibited by default and a Health Canada CTLS or CRA Excise audit of `ORG_CANNABIS_NURSERY_PROC` (Brockville) has zero visibility into `salvage` tables or Kemptville automotive cash sales.
* **NFR-REL-01 (Tenant-Partitioned Offline Queue Durability):** The Yard & Cultivation PWA shall queue binary photo blobs and `CustodyEvent` mutations in IndexedDB (`Dexie.js`) partitioned by `organizationId`, display a persistent high-contrast badge showing the count of unsynced items, and block user logout, workspace switching, or cache clearing while unsynced mutations exist.
* **NFR-IDEM-01 (End-to-End Idempotency):** Every barcode scan, offline batch sync, eBay webhook, and WhatsApp webhook must supply a deterministic `Idempotency-Key` enforced by PostgreSQL unique constraints so network retries or double-taps never duplicate records.
* **NFR-ACC-01 (Gloved & Outdoor Usability):** All mobile scanner, nursery, and Vault POS screens must conform to WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios ($> 4.5:1$), use minimum $56 \times 56\text{ px}$ touch targets for gloved operation, and pair every color indicator (red/green) with distinct text, icon, and audio/haptic cues.
